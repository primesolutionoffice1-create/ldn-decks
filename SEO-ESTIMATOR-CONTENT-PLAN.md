## Internal Linking Rules

- Link to `/deck-payment-estimator` once in the body when the reader is actively evaluating cost or financing.
- Use varied anchors: `estimate your monthly deck payment`, `deck payment calculator`, `payment estimator`, `estimate deck costs`, `compare monthly payment options`.
- Do not add sitewide footer/header links unless conversion data proves estimator usage is strong.
- Link from estimator back to the strongest cost, material, financing, and contact pages.

## Production Notes

- Keep examples realistic and qualify all numbers as estimates.
- Avoid promises about approval, rates, loan terms, or tax/home value impact.
- Use local proof when available: project examples, county permit context, HOA process, manufacturer credentials, and real review themes.
