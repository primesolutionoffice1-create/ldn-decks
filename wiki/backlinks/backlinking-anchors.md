# Backlinking Anchors

When building backlinks to your website, the anchor text you use plays a critical role in how search engines perceive those links. Unlike internal anchor text, external anchor text requires a more diverse and natural approach to avoid triggering spam signals

The most important principle for external anchor text is to make your link profile look organic and natural. Professional SEO work should be invisible – your links should appear as if they were earned naturally through great content and genuine recommendations.

Key Guidelines to Follow

Avoid Repetition  
Never use the same anchor text repeatedly across multiple external links. Repetitive anchor text is one of the strongest spam signals to search engines. The only exception is for exact match keywords, which you might repeat once or twice – but only when you have 20-40+ total backlinks to dilute the pattern.

Natural Keyword Integration  
When incorporating keywords into your anchor text:

* Ensure they flow naturally within the surrounding content  
* Place links within relevant sections that discuss the destination page's topic  
* Match the anchor text meaning to the destination URL's content

💡 Remember: Google analyzes the content surrounding your link, not just the anchor text itself. Context matters\!

Strategic Placement  
Always place external links within the main body content of the page. Avoid:

* Footer links  
* Sidebar links  
* Links within heading tags

Body content links carry the most weight and appear most natural to search engines.

The 30-20-30-5-15 Formula

For a natural-looking external link profile, follow this distribution:

30% Branded Anchor Text

Use your business name, website name, or brand variations. This builds brand recognition and looks completely natural.

20% Generic Anchor Text

Include phrases like "click here," "read more," or "visit this site." While we avoid these for internal links, they're essential for external link diversity.

30% Keyword-Rich Partial Match

Add extra words to your target keywords to create longer, more natural phrases. For example, if targeting "plumber Dallas," use "experienced plumber in Dallas area" or "Dallas plumber services near me."

5% Exact Match

Use your exact target keyword sparingly. This is your most powerful but also most dangerous anchor text type. Limit to 2-3 instances maximum, even with many backlinks.

15% Natural Variations

Use synonyms and related terms without the exact keyword. For instance, use "attorney" instead of "lawyer" or "dental practice" instead of "dentist."

⚠️ Warning: Over-optimization of external anchor text is one of the fastest ways to trigger a Google penalty. When in doubt, err on the side of being too natural rather than too optimized.

Comparing Internal vs. External Anchor Text

📌 Key Differences from Internal Linking:

* Internal links should avoid generic text; external links need it  
* Internal links can be more keyword-focused; external links require more variety  
* Internal links shouldn't use naked URLs; external links benefit from them  
* Internal links build site structure; external links build authority

⏩⏩ For a refresher on internal linking strategies, review the [Internal Anchor Text Guidelines](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=892b004d3b5d46448803e50528ca0536) lesson in the Creating AI Content module.

These percentages serve as guidelines, not rigid rules. Your primary focus should always be creating a link profile that appears natural and provides value to users across the web.
