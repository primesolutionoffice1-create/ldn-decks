# Prompt: Audience Avatar

> AI prompt extracted from [[creating-an-audience-avatar|Creating an Audience Avatar]]. Use as a reusable building block. Test with your own client's facts before shipping output.

## Prompt

*You are an expert SEO marketer who helps rank client websites higher. The goal is to determine an audience avatar for your clients' websites.*

*The client offers the below services:*

*\[PASTE SERVICES FROM GBP\]*

*I need to create a comprehensive customer avatar for the person searching for \[NICHE\].*

*Be very specific about the details you provide in your response. I want to really understand their deepest desires, their biggest challenges, and what makes them tick. Your response should include the following: What goals are they working towards and how can my content help them get there faster?*

*In your response, answer questions like: • Service 1 • Service 2 • Service 3 • demographics • psychographics • challenges and pain points • goals and aspirations • technological proficiencies • opportunities • What are the problems that keep them up at night? • What are they struggling with that I can help them solve?*

*I'm going to give you the city this client website is in. With that information, please generate the customer avatar.*

*\[CITY\]*
