# GBP Categories Prompt

> AI prompt extracted from [[gbp-categories|GBP Categories]]. Use as a reusable building block. Test with your own client's facts before shipping output.

## Prompt

You are a local seo expert. What additional categories should a local business \[niche\] use for their Google Business Profile? I've given you a list of all current GBP categories. Please choose 3 or 4 additional relevant categories from this list. Here are the services they're currently using:

\[Services\]

---

Why Multiple Categories Matter

Think of GBP categories as different doors into your client's business. Each category represents a different way customers might search for their services. When you only use one category, you're essentially keeping all but one door locked.

💡 Key Insight: While it's easier to rank for broader categories, using multiple specific categories helps you capture more targeted, high-intent searches.

The Smart Approach to Category Selection

Instead of manually reviewing Google's thousand-plus categories, we'll use AI to streamline the process:

Step 1: Gather Your Information

Collect the following details about your client:

* Business type and primary service  
* Location  
* Complete list of services offered

Step 2: Use AI for Category Recommendations

Here's the efficient method we use:

1. Input your client's business description (e.g., "HVAC service installation company in Houston")  
2. Add their complete service list  
3. Use ChatGPT to generate relevant GBP category suggestions

This approach transforms what could be hours of research into a quick, targeted list of appropriate categories.

Step 3: Review and Select Categories

From the AI-generated list:

* Start at the top and work your way down  
* Verify each category against actual services provided  
* Select categories that accurately represent your client's offerings

📌 Important Rule: Choose 5 secondary categories plus your primary category (6 total)

Best Practices for Category Selection

When reviewing potential categories, ask yourself:

* Does the client actually provide this service?  
* Is this how customers would search for the business?  
* Does this category complement the primary category?

⚠️ Warning: Never select categories for services your client doesn't actually provide. This can harm their credibility and potentially lead to GBP suspension.
