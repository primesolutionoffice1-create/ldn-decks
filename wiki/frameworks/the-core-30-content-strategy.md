# The Core 30 Content Strategy

Before diving into the Core 30 concept, it's crucial to understand a fundamental principle of local SEO that sets it apart from traditional SEO strategies. In local SEO, every single piece of content on your website exists for one primary purpose—to rank your Google Business Profile (GBP) higher.

This represents a significant shift in mindset from traditional SEO. While conventional SEO often focuses on ranking individual pages for specific keywords, local SEO takes a holistic approach. Every URL, every article, every page on your site works together as part of a larger ecosystem designed to establish your business as the go-to authority in your local area.

💡 Key Insight: In local SEO, we're not trying to rank individual articles or pages. Everything we create is designed to build topical or geographical relevance that elevates your GBP in local search results. This unified approach is what makes local SEO both powerful and efficient.

The Power of Topical Relevance

Building a successful local SEO presence follows a specific hierarchy that cannot be shortcuts. You must establish topical relevance before geographical relevance—this is non-negotiable. Think of it as building a house: you need a solid foundation before you can add walls and a roof.

Why Topical Relevance Comes First

Consider this practical example: You can't establish yourself as "Chicago's best plumber" if Google doesn't first understand that you're a plumber. Without strong topical signals, any geographical signals you send become meaningless. It's like trying to be famous for something you haven't proven you can do.

The most effective way to build this topical relevance is through strategic content creation that mirrors your business's actual services and expertise. This is where the Core 30 strategy becomes not just useful, but essential to your local SEO success.

📚 Related Learning: To understand how Google evaluates topical relevance and authority, review the lesson [[how-seo-authority-is-passed|How SEO Authority is Passed]] 

**What Exactly is the Core 30?**

The Core 30 is a strategic content framework that creates perfect alignment between your website structure and your Google Business Profile. It's called the "Core" 30 because these pages form the absolute foundation of your local SEO presence—without them, other efforts will yield limited results.

Core Components Breakdown

1\. Category Pages (3-5 pages)

* One comprehensive page for each primary category in your GBP  
* These are your broadest service offerings (e.g., "Plumbing Services," "HVAC Services")  
* Each page should contain 1,500-2,500 words of in-depth content  
* Focus on explaining what the category encompasses and why it matters

2\. Service Pages (20-30 pages)

* One detailed page for each specific service listed in your GBP  
* These drill down into specific offerings (e.g., "Water Heater Repair," "Drain Cleaning")  
* Each page should contain 1,000-2,000 words of targeted content  
* Include specific information about processes, benefits, and common customer questions

3\. Essential Business Pages

* About Us: Establishes your business story, credentials, and local connection  
* Contact Us: Provides clear pathways for customer engagement  
* Additional pages as needed (e.g., service areas, testimonials)

This typically totals around 30-40 pages—hence the name "Core 30" (because, let's be honest, "Core 40" doesn't have the same ring to it\!).

🎯 Strategic Note: The exact number isn't as important as the concept. Whether you end up with 28 or 35 pages, what matters is that you have comprehensive coverage of every service and category in your GBP.

Why the Core 30 Works So Effectively

The Core 30 strategy succeeds because it leverages multiple SEO principles simultaneously:

1\. Content Volume and Depth

When properly executed, the Core 30 creates tens of thousands of words of topically relevant content. This massive content foundation sends strong signals to Google about your expertise and comprehensive service offerings. It's not just about quantity—it's about creating a complete topical map of your business.

2\. Perfect Structural Alignment

Your website structure becomes a mirror image of your GBP structure. This alignment makes it incredibly easy for Google to understand:

* What services you offer  
* How those services relate to each other  
* Which services are most important to your business  
* How comprehensive your expertise is in each area

3\. Internal Linking Opportunities

With 30+ interconnected pages, you create natural opportunities for internal linking that:

* Distributes authority throughout your site  
* Helps users navigate between related services  
* Reinforces topical relationships  
* Keeps visitors engaged longer

4\. User Experience Benefits

Visitors can easily find detailed information about any service they're interested in, improving:

* Time on site metrics  
* Conversion rates  
* Customer confidence  
* Overall user satisfaction

Monitoring Your Progress: The Data-Driven Approach

Building the Core 30 isn't a "set it and forget it" strategy. Success requires careful monitoring and adjustment based on real performance data.

Setting Up Your Tracking System

1. Implement Rank Tracking  
   * Use professional rank tracking tools to monitor local visibility  
   * Track rankings for your primary service \+ location combinations  
   * Monitor both GBP rankings and organic website rankings  
   * Set up alerts for significant ranking changes  
2. Automate Rank Maps  
   * Schedule weekly or bi-weekly rank map reports  
   * These visual representations show your visibility radius  
   * Watch how your coverage area expands as you publish content  
   * Identify gaps in coverage that need attention  
3. Track Publishing Impact  
   * Document when each Core 30 page goes live  
   * Note ranking changes 2-4 weeks after publishing  
   * Identify which pages create the biggest impact  
   * Use this data to prioritize remaining content

⚡ Success Note: Here's an exciting reality—the Core 30 strategy is so powerful that over 80% of local businesses achieve their ranking goals before even completing all 30 pages\! Many businesses see significant improvements after publishing just 10-15 pages, especially if they start with their most important categories and services.

Critical Success Factor: Link Building

While creating the Core 30 content is crucial, remember that content alone isn't enough in competitive markets. The two-pronged approach of content \+ links is what creates unstoppable local SEO momentum.

Why Links Matter for the Core 30

* Authority Distribution: Links pass authority to your Core 30 pages, making them more likely to influence your GBP rankings  
* Crawl Priority: Linked pages get crawled and indexed faster  
* Trust Signals: Quality links indicate that other websites vouch for your content  
* Competitive Edge: In competitive markets, links often make the difference between ranking \#3 and ranking \#1

Link Building Best Practices for Core 30

1. Prioritize Your Most Important Pages  
   * Start with category pages (they're broader and easier to link to)  
   * Focus on your highest-revenue services  
   * Build links gradually and naturally  
2. Diversify Your Link Sources  
   * Local business directories  
   * Industry-specific directories  
   * Local news and blog mentions  
   * Partner and vendor websites  
   * Community organization sites  
3. Monitor Link Impact  
   * Track which pages have links  
   * Measure ranking improvements after link acquisition  
   * Adjust strategy based on results

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Understanding what not to do is just as important as knowing what to do:

1\. Publishing Without Optimization

Don't just create content for the sake of hitting a number. Each page should be:

* Fully optimized for its target service/category  
* Written with user intent in mind  
* Properly formatted with headers, bullets, and images  
* Mobile-friendly and fast-loading

2\. Ignoring Content Quality

AI can help create content quickly, but don't sacrifice quality for speed:

* Always edit AI-generated content  
* Add local examples and case studies  
* Include unique insights from your business experience  
* Ensure accuracy and helpfulness

3\. Forgetting the User

While the Core 30 is designed to rank your GBP, remember that real people will read these pages:

* Write in a conversational, approachable tone  
* Answer common customer questions  
* Include clear calls-to-action  
* Make it easy to contact you

Homepage vs. GBP Landing Page

Your approach here depends on your business structure:

Single Location Businesses:

* Homepage \= GBP Landing Page  
* Target keyword: \[GBP Primary Category\] \+ \[City Name\]  
* Example: "Plumber Houston" or "Plumber Chicago"

Multi-Location Businesses:

* Homepage remains separate  
* Create individual GBP landing pages for each location  
* Each landing page targets its specific location

💡 Pro Tip: Your title tag and H1 should include your exact match target keyword, but you can add additional words for context. Example: "Find Out Why We're the Best Plumber in Chicago 2025"

The Core 30 Structure Breakdown

1\. GBP Landing Page (Homepage for Single Locations)

Your GBP landing page serves as the hub for all your services and categories. Structure it with:

* Target Keyword: \[Primary Category\] \+ \[City Name\]  
* Subheadings: One for each GBP category (up to 10 total)  
* Content Blocks: 50-100 words under each subheading  
* Internal Links: Link from each block to its dedicated category/service page

2\. Category Pages

Create one URL for each GBP category:

* Primary Category: Gets its own dedicated page  
* Secondary Categories: Up to 9 additional category pages  
* Target Keywords: \[Category Name\] \+ \[City Name\]  
  ⏩⏩ Related Lesson: Review [GBP Categories](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=c326cb348dbb41b5a1c946596b9e1f88) to understand how to select and optimize your Google Business Profile categories effectively.

3\. Service Pages

Build individual pages for each service you offer:

* Minimum Recommendation: 11+ service pages  
* Target Keywords: \[Service Name\] \+ \[City Name\]  
* Example: "Water Heater Replacement Houston"

Special High-Priority Services

For critical, high-margin services (limit to 2-3):

* Link directly from the homepage  
* Track rankings separately in your rank tracking tool  
* Common examples for plumbers:  
  * Water heater replacement  
  * Main drain line replacement

4\. Locations Page

A standard locations page listing all your service areas or business locations.

Beyond the Core 30: Building Relevance

Once your Core 30 is complete, evaluate your rank map to determine next steps:

If your geographic coverage is weak, add supporting content (colored green in the diagram.)

* Source: People Also Ask questions  
* Structure: \[PAA Question\] \+ \[City Name\]  
* Example: "How Much Money Does a Plumber Make in Houston?"  
* Linking: Connect these pages to relevant service pages

When topical relevance is strong but you need broader geographic coverage (purple in diagram):

* Target Keywords: \[Primary Category/Service\] \+ \[Geographic Landmark\]  
* Examples:  
  * "Plumber River Oaks" (neighborhood)  
  * "Plumber I-10 and 610 Interchange" (major landmark)  
* Purpose: Expand your visibility across the service area

Multi-Location Considerations

For businesses with multiple locations, the Core 30 multiplies:

* 3 Locations \= Core 90: Build complete structures for each location  
* Prioritize Based on Rank Maps: You might not need to complete all 30 pages for every location  
* Watch for Patterns: Location 2 might achieve good topical relevance before Location 3 finishes its Core 30

⚡ Efficiency Tip: Use your rank tracking data to guide content priorities. Don't build unnecessary pages if your topical relevance is already strong.
