# Property Audit Part 2

After completing your Core 30 content and building external links to those pages, you might find your rank map still showing room for improvement. This is a clear signal that you need to build more topical relevance for your target keywords.

Topical relevance means creating content that comprehensively covers your subject matter. Google rewards websites that demonstrate deep expertise in their topic areas. The most effective way to build this relevance is by answering the questions Google already knows people are asking.

![[images/image18.png]]

Finding Your Questions

To identify the most relevant questions for your topic:

1. **Search without geographic modifiers** \- If you're ranking a plumber in Houston, search just "plumber"  
2. **Locate the People Also Ask section** \- It typically appears in the middle of search results  
3. **Trigger more questions** \- If PAA doesn't appear, add "how" or "what" to your search  
4. **Expand your question list** \- Click to open and close questions repeatedly to reveal dozens more

💡 **Pro Tip**: Opening and closing PAA questions multiple times can reveal 20-30+ related questions that Google considers highly relevant to your topic.

Creating Content from PAA Questions

The strategy has evolved since summer 2023\. Here's the current best practice:

Step 1: Rewrite Questions Semantically

Instead of using PAA questions as exact-match H2 headings, use AI to rewrite them in a semantically relevant way while adding your city name.

**Example:**

* Original PAA: "How much does a plumber make?"  
* Rewritten H2: "How Much Money Does a Plumber Make in Houston?"

Step 2: Answer Immediately and Succinctly

The first sentence after your H2 should deliver a direct answer:

H2: How Much Money Does a Plumber Make in Houston?

A plumber in Houston makes between $75,000 and $120,000 per year on average...

Notice how we repeat key elements from the question in our answer. This helps Google's algorithm clearly understand that we're providing a direct answer to the specific question.

Step 3: Create Supporting Long-Form Content

After your initial 50-75 word answer paragraph, link to a comprehensive 1,500-word article that fully explores the topic. This article should:

* Be specific to your location (Houston in our example)  
* Cover various aspects (experience levels, license types, specializations)  
* Include a contextual editorial link back to the original FAQ page  
* Have its own external backlink

Implementation Schedule

Monthly Content Production

* Target 3-5 supporting articles per month per topic  
* Alternate between topics each month  
* Allow time for ranking improvements between content batches

**Example Schedule:**

* Month 1: 3-5 articles about "plumber"  
* Month 2: 3-5 articles about "water heater replacement"  
* Month 3: Return to "plumber" articles  
* Continue alternating...

Why Alternate Topics?

Ranking improvements take time to manifest. By alternating between topics monthly, you:

* Avoid over-producing content for topics that may already have sufficient relevance  
* Give Google time to process and rank your new content  
* Can measure the impact of your efforts before investing more resources

⚡ **Quick Check**: After implementing topical relevance content, monitor your rank map. When you see significant improvement across your target keywords, it's time to shift focus to [[content-planning-geographical-relevance-part-2|Building More Geographical Relevance.]]
