# Content Planning: Topical Relevance Part 1

In the modern Google algorithm, **topical relevance is more important than keyword research**. While keywords remain relevant, topical relevance is how you establish your website as the comprehensive authority on your target subject.

Topical relevance means covering all aspects of a topic area. Google analyzes the breadth and depth of your content to determine if you're truly an expert in your field.

**🔑 Key Insight:** You're building topical clusters that signal to Google your site is the go-to resource for everything related to your niche.

Using AI for Topic Generation

When requesting topics from AI, specify that they should be **"semantically relevant according to Google's algorithm"** \- not just what the AI considers relevant.

This distinction matters because Google's algorithm has specific semantic understanding that differs from general AI associations.

Implementation Process

⚠️ Always use the most current AI model available, and verify you have the correct model selected before proceeding.

1. **Select the newest AI model available**  
2. **Use the provided prompt in this module** (don't modify the semantic relevance specification)  
3. **Generate 25 related topics** for your target subject  
4. **Review results** for Google algorithm alignment

Next Steps

Use your 25 topically relevant subjects to plan content creation, identify gaps, build topic clusters, and establish semantic authority.
