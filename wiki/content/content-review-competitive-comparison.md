Page Optimizer Pro's Functionality

Page Optimizer Pro analyzes your content against your competitors', focusing on the usage of keywords and LSI keywords. Implementing Recommendations

After setting up your project and defining your main keyword—alongside any secondary keywords if applicable—the tool processes your input and outputs a score indicating the current optimization level of your content. For example, a score of 25% suggests significant room for improvement.

The tool will offer detailed guidance on where and how to use critical terms effectively. This includes suggestions for your title tags, subheadings, and the main body of your content. If the tool indicates an overuse of a particular term, it will advise on reducing its frequency to avoid keyword stuffing, which can negatively impact SEO.

Applying SEO Enhancements

The report also extends beyond mere keyword usage. It assesses other elements such as the use of bold and italics, the inclusion of images, lists, tables, and even the implementation of structured data (schema). By comparing your site’s schema with that of your competitors, Page Optimizer Pro can suggest additional types of schema that may enhance your page's visibility and relevance.

Leveraging Page Structure and Content

Lastly, based on the optimization report, you’ll make the necessary adjustments to your content. This could involve rewriting sections to better align with the recommended keyword distribution and incorporating suggested HTML tags and schema. These changes are typically minor but can significantly impact how effectively Google indexes and ranks your page.
