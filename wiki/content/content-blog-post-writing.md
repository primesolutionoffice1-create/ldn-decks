# Content: Blog Post Writing

Now that you've created your outline, it's time for the final step: getting Claude to write the actual content. This lesson will walk you through the process of generating high-quality, human-like content that ranks well and passes AI detection tests.

Before Claude starts writing, you need to provide two essential components:

1\. Your Audience Avatar

Use the same avatar you created in the [Creating an Audience Avatar](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=01b1a43404dc4b2192bc3bd3dbc63632) lesson. This ensures your content speaks directly to your target audience and maintains consistency across all your materials.

2\. Writing Samples

Writing samples are crucial for teaching Claude the style and structure that performs well in search results. Here's how to choose them:

* **If you have other ranking clients**: Use content from those successful campaigns  
* **If you're just starting**: Find high-ranking content in any niche through Google searches  
* **Format**: Copy the content into a Word document or similar format  
* **Why this works**: Google rewards content similar to what's already ranking well

💡 **Pro Tip**: Your writing samples don't need to be from the same industry. Focus on finding content that ranks well and engages readers, regardless of the niche.
