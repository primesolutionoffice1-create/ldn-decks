# Content : Blog Post Outline

Creating a well-structured outline is the foundation of compelling AI-generated content. In this lesson, you'll learn how to use Claude's custom projects feature to create blog outlines that engage readers and improve your SEO performance.

Before you ask Claude to write any content, you need to establish the structure of your article. This structure serves as the blueprint that guides Claude in creating content that not only ranks well but also keeps readers engaged from start to finish.

💡 **Remember**: Better engagement metrics \= higher rankings on search engine results pages

The Recommended Blog Structure

Here's the proven structure we use for creating high-performing blog articles:

1\. The Hook

Start with something compelling and engaging that immediately captures attention. This opening needs to make readers want to continue reading your article.

2\. The Sneak Peek

Give readers a preview of what they'll learn in the article. This sets expectations and helps readers decide if your content addresses their needs.

3\. The Story

People connect with stories. Including a relevant narrative helps maintain attention and makes your content more memorable. Claude will provide you with seven different storytelling approaches to choose from.

4\. Main Points

This is where you deliver the core value of your article—the information your readers came to find.

5\. Call to Action (CTA)

End with a clear next step for your readers, whether that's contacting your business, reading another article, or taking another desired action.

📝 **Note**: This structure isn't set in stone. Feel free to adapt it based on your needs or your clients' preferences. The key is having a clear structure before you start writing.

Working with Claude: Step-by-Step Process

When creating an outline with Claude, you'll follow an interactive process that allows you to maintain control while leveraging AI's creative capabilities.

Step 1: Provide Basic Information

Start by giving Claude:

* Your target keyword (e.g., "roofer")  
* Your target city (e.g., "San Antonio")

Step 2: Choose Your Hook

Claude will present you with five different hook options. Review them and select the one that best resonates with your target audience.

Step 3: Define the Sneak Peek Questions

You have two options:

* Provide Claude with three specific questions you want to address  
* Let Claude suggest three questions based on common search intent

💡 **Pro Tip**: If you're unsure what questions to address, let Claude make suggestions—it's trained on common search patterns and user intent.

Step 4: Select Your Story Approach

Claude will provide seven different storytelling methods, such as:

* Personal anecdotes  
* Client success stories  
* Metaphors  
* Historical examples  
* Problem/solution narratives

Choose the approach that best fits your brand voice and audience.

Step 5: Review and Copy the Complete Outline

Once you've made all your selections, Claude will generate the complete outline in one place. Copy this outline—you'll need it for the content writing phase.

Customizing Your Outline Structure

The beauty of using Claude with custom instructions is that you can create any structure that works for your specific needs.

How to Modify the Structure

1. **Start with your ideal article example**: If you have existing content that performs well, use it as a template  
2. **Ask Claude for help**: Copy your current custom instructions and ask Claude to help you modify them  
3. **Be specific about changes**: Want to add an FAQ section? Include local business information? Just tell Claude exactly what you want

Example Custom Structure Request:

"I want to modify this outline structure to include:  
\- An FAQ section at the end  
\- More emphasis on local relevance  
\- A section for customer testimonials"

Claude will then provide you with updated custom instructions that reflect your desired structure.

✅✅✅ Do:

* Always review and approve each section before moving forward  
* Keep your target audience (from your [Audience Avatar](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=01b1a43404dc4b2192bc3bd3dbc63632)) in mind when making selections  
* Save successful outlines as templates for similar content  
* Experiment with different structures for different content types

❌❌❌ Don't:

* Rush through the selections—each choice impacts the final content quality  
* Use the same hook or story approach for every article  
* Forget to copy the final outline before moving to the content creation phase
