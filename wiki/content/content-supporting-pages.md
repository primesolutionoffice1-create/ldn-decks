# Content: Supporting Pages

Supporting content exists not as standalone material but as a means to boost another URL's rank. Instead of merely increasing the number of backlinks to a target URL—a method with diminishing returns—supporting content enhances the content ecosystem around the target URL. This is critical because Google favors URLs that engage users, rewarding similar content that satisfies searcher intent.

Writing Supporting Content

Creating high-quality supporting content involves identifying topics that add depth and detailed answers to common questions within your niche. This can be achieved through AI-generated FAQs, which we will explore in a subsequent module. These FAQs help in pinpointing relevant questions and providing detailed answers, which are then expanded into full articles.

Practical Application

To put this into practice, consider generating between one to ten supporting articles each month based on competitive need, with three being a typical number due to cost-effectiveness and sufficient impact.

Each piece of supporting content should link to your target URL and vice versa. This interlinking helps in building a robust content structure that Google recognizes as thematically cohesive.

![[images/image26.png]]

As you continue to add content monthly, you maintain this interconnected structure, ensuring that each new piece integrates seamlessly, further strengthening the topical silo around your main target.

The Impact of Supporting Content

This methodology, revolving around the creation and strategic use of supporting content, constitutes approximately 80% of the ongoing SEO efforts. If a target URL does not rank or attract traffic, the solution often involves publishing more supporting content.

Conversely, if a target URL performs well, the strategy shifts to developing new supporting content for other URLs.
