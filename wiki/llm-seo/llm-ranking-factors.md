# LLM Ranking Factors

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer

> See **[[2026-ai-overviews-state|2026 AI Overviews State]]** for current state.

![[llm-seo-ranking-factors.svg]]

When it comes to ranking in ChatGPT recommendations, proving your business is legitimate isn't about having the proper paperwork filed away in your office. ChatGPT evaluates business legitimacy by examining third-party sources across the internet that it already trusts, then determining whether it can infer credibility for your business based on where you're mentioned online.

ChatGPT asks key questions when evaluating your business:

* Does this business exist on platforms that ChatGPT trusts?  
* Do multiple reliable sources point to the same consistent information?  
* Is the business information accurate and consistent across different platforms?

This evaluation process makes consistency absolutely critical. When your business appears on high-authority platforms with consistent information, ChatGPT gains confidence in your legitimacy.

Super Citations: Your Legitimacy Foundation

What I call "super citations" are high-authority business directory listings that carry significant weight with ChatGPT. Unlike average, low-quality business directories, these super citations have major influence across multiple platforms:

* Google Search and Maps  
* ChatGPT recommendations  
* Voice assistants (Alexa, Siri, etc.)  
* GPS navigation systems  
* Other AI-powered recommendation engines

These citations give your business credibility across the entire digital ecosystem, not just individual platforms.
