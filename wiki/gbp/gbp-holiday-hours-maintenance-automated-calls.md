# GBP Holiday Hours Maintenance + Automated Calls

Keeping your Google Business Profile (GBP) holiday hours updated is a small but critical task that signals to Google that your business is actively managed and cares about customer experience.

Google uses special hours as one of its signals to determine if a business is actively maintaining their GBP. An up-to-date profile with accurate holiday hours shows:

* Your business is legitimate and operational  
* You care about customer experience  
* Your GBP is actively managed (not abandoned)

This seemingly minor detail contributes to your overall local SEO authority and can impact your visibility in local search results. Just like maintaining your GBP Services and other profile elements, holiday hours are part of demonstrating consistent, professional management.

![[images/image24.png]]  
How to Update Holiday Hours

Step-by-Step Process

1. Access Your GBP Dashboard  
   * Log into your Google Business Profile (must be verified first)  
   * Click "Edit profile"  
   * Scroll down to "Hours"  
2. Find Special Hours  
   * Look for the "Special hours" section  
   * Click the pencil icon to edit  
3. Add Holiday Hours  
   * Select the holiday date  
   * Set your hours (or mark as closed)  
   * Save your changes

⚠️ Important Limitation

Google only allows you to set special hours 2-3 months in advance. You cannot set Christmas hours in July or Thanksgiving hours in August. This limitation requires a proactive management approach.

![[images/image25.png]]  
Creating a Management System

Since Google restricts how far in advance you can set holiday hours, you need a systematic approach to keep them updated throughout the year. This type of proactive management is exactly what helps reduce client churn by demonstrating ongoing value and attention to detail.

📅 Recommended Schedule

Set a recurring calendar reminder for the 1st of every other month to:

* Review upcoming holidays  
* Update all client GBPs with special hours  
* Verify previously entered hours are still accurate

Common Holidays to Track

* New Year's Day  
* Martin Luther King Jr. Day  
* Presidents' Day  
* Memorial Day  
* Independence Day (4th of July)  
* Labor Day  
* Columbus Day  
* Veterans Day  
* Thanksgiving  
* Christmas Eve  
* Christmas Day  
* New Year's Eve

💡 Pro Tip: Some businesses may also need to account for religious holidays, local events, or industry-specific closures. Know your client's schedule\!

Small details like holiday hours might seem insignificant, but they're part of the larger picture of maintaining an optimized, trustworthy online presence for local businesses.

Google Business Profile includes a feature for automated calls that's designed to keep your business information current. However, this feature has been known to cause issues—including potential suspensions of your GBP.

We've experienced firsthand that GBPs can get suspended due to automated calls. To protect your profile and maintain control over your business information, it's best to turn this feature off completely.

How to Turn Off Automated Calls

Follow these simple steps to disable automated calls:

1. Access Business Profile Settings  
   * Navigate to your Google Business Profile  
   * Click the three dots menu (⋮)  
   * Select "Business hours"  
2. Navigate to Advanced Settings  
   * Look for and click on "Advanced settings"  
3. Disable Automated Calls  
   * Find "Automated calls to keep your business profile up to date"  
   * Toggle the switch to turn it OFF

That's it\! This quick adjustment helps ensure your GBP stays active and under your control.
