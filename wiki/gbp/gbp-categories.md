# GBP Categories

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Strategy unchanged — but use current Claude/GPT models for category prompts.
> See **[[2026-gbp-changes|2026 GBP Changes]]** for current state.

One of the most common oversights we encounter is an underutilized Google Business Profile (GBP) category setup. Most businesses select their primary category and stop there—leaving significant ranking opportunities on the table.

Google offers over 1,000 different business categories, each providing a unique opportunity to appear in relevant local searches. By strategically selecting multiple categories, you expand your client's visibility across various search terms their potential customers might use.
