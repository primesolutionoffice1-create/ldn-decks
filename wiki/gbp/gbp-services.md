# GBP Services

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Strategy unchanged — but use current Claude/GPT models for service prompts.
> See **[[2026-gbp-changes|2026 GBP Changes]]** for current state.

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) services section is a crucial component that helps potential customers understand exactly what you offer. This section appears prominently on your GBP listing and directly influences how Google matches your business with relevant searches.

Services in your GBP accomplish three key objectives:

1. Search Visibility \- Each service you list becomes a potential keyword match for customer searches  
2. Customer Clarity \- Visitors immediately understand your offerings without clicking through to your website  
3. Category Enhancement \- Services work hand-in-hand with your GBP categories to strengthen your local relevance

Strategic Service Selection

When adding services to your GBP, you're not just listing what you do—you're strategically positioning your business for maximum visibility. The services you choose will directly inform your category selection process, creating a cohesive profile that Google's algorithm can easily understand and rank.

💡 Pro Tip: Don't limit yourself to exactly 20 services. Ask for more if needed—comprehensive service listings help capture more search variations. In our example, asking for 20 services resulted in 46 relevant options\!
