## Decision Criteria
- Slope and yard conditions.
- Height from the home and access needs.
- Drainage, footings, and permit complexity.
- Maintenance expectations.
- Resale and visual impact.
- Integration with railings, stairs, lighting, kitchens, and covered structures.
