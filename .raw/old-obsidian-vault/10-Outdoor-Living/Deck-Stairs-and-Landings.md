## Stair Context

Deck stair systems connected with
[[Deck-Builder-Northern-Virginia]]
must comply with safety and code requirements documented in
[[Virginia-Deck-Code-Guide]]
and
[[Deck-Permits-Virginia]].

Stair projects frequently connect with
[[Deck-Railings]],
[[Cable-Railings]],
and
[[Deck-Framing-and-Accessories]]
because framing, railing spacing,
and structural support must work together.

Luxury outdoor living upgrades commonly relate to
[[Deck-Lighting]]
and
[[Custom-Deck-Design]]
for integrated stair lighting and premium finishes.
