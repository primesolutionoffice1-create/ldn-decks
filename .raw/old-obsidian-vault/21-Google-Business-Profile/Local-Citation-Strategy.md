## Primary Semantic Entity

Local citation strategy strengthens business consistency, local entity trust, Maps visibility, brand authority, and service-area confidence for Loudoun Decks.

## Citation Goals

Local citations should reinforce:
- consistent business name
- service area
- phone number
- website
- category relevance
- contractor trust
- local authority

## Citation Context

## NAP Consistency Rules

- Use the same business name everywhere
- Use the same phone number everywhere
- Use the same website URL everywhere
- Keep service categories consistent
- Add project photos where possible
- Add service descriptions with local terms
- Link to relevant service pages where allowed
