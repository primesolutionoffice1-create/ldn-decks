## Trex

### Strengths
- Strong brand recognition
- Wide contractor network
- Good fade resistance
- Multiple pricing tiers
- Strong warranty reputation
- Large color selection

### Weaknesses
- Boards can retain heat
- Lower product lines may scratch easier
- Premium lines can become expensive
- Some homeowners dislike board texture

## TimberTech

### Strengths
- Premium aesthetics
- Strong PVC product lines
- Better heat performance in some collections
- Good moisture resistance
- Luxury appearance

### Weaknesses
- Higher price point
- Some collections require careful installation
- Premium PVC lines can be costly
- Availability varies by region

## Fiberon

### Strengths
- Competitive pricing
- Good color variety
- Lower maintenance
- Eco-focused branding
- Good value for mid-range budgets

### Weaknesses
- Less premium perception
- Fewer luxury board options
- Contractor familiarity varies
- Some product lines have limited texture realism

## MoistureShield

### Strengths
- Strong moisture resistance
- Suitable near water
- Solid-core technology
- Good durability for humid environments

### Weaknesses
- Smaller contractor ecosystem
- Fewer style options
- Limited market awareness
- Premium pricing on higher-end boards

## Deckorators

### Strengths
- Mineral-based composite options
- Lightweight boards
- Good stability
- Modern aesthetics
- Strong traction performance

### Weaknesses
- Less widespread availability
- Premium lines can be expensive
- Smaller product ecosystem
- Fewer long-term consumer reviews
