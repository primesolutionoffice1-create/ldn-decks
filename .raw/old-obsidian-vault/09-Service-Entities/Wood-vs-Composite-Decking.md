## Wood Deck Advantages
- Lower upfront cost
- Natural appearance
- Easier board replacement
- Traditional deck aesthetics
- Wide stain color flexibility

## Composite Deck Advantages
- Lower maintenance
- Longer lifespan
- Better moisture resistance
- Reduced fading
- No staining requirements
- Premium appearance options
