## FAQ

### Is TimberTech PVC decking?
Some TimberTech collections use advanced PVC decking technology while others use capped composite systems.

### Does TimberTech get hot?
Like most decking materials, darker boards may retain more heat in direct sunlight.

### Is TimberTech better than Trex?
The best option depends on maintenance goals, aesthetics, pricing, warranty preferences, and heat performance priorities.

### How long does TimberTech decking last?
Many TimberTech systems are designed for long-term durability and low maintenance.

### Can TimberTech boards fade?
Modern TimberTech collections offer improved fade protection compared to older composite decking systems.
