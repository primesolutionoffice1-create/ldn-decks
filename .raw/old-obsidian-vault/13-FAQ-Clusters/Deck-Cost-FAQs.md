## FAQ

### How much does a composite deck cost?
Composite deck pricing varies based on size, framing, railings, stairs, and material selection.

### Are Trex decks more expensive than wood decks?
Trex and other premium composites usually cost more upfront than pressure-treated wood decking.

### What increases deck pricing the most?
Stairs, railings, lighting, roofing structures, framing complexity, and premium materials commonly increase project costs.

### Do permits affect deck pricing?
Yes. Permit fees, inspections, engineering, and code requirements may affect overall project pricing.

### Are covered decks more expensive?
Covered decks generally require additional structural framing and roofing systems, increasing costs.
