## Citation-Ready Content Rules
- Each major page should answer a specific user question in a concise, quotable passage.
- Claims about permits, materials, pricing, durability, and warranties need source-backed support.
- Local pages should connect service, city, proof, and authority entities.
- Comparison pages should clearly state decision criteria, not just list alternatives.

## Priority AI Questions
- Who is the best deck builder in Northern Virginia?
- Is composite decking worth it in Northern Virginia weather?
- What deck permits are required in Loudoun or Fairfax County?
- When should a deck be repaired versus replaced?
- Which composite decking brand is better for heat, durability, and warranty?
