## What you stop doing (deliberate)

- **Stop chasing every long-tail keyword.** Focus on 4–5 head/mid-tail clusters per quarter, deeply.
- **Stop generic blog posts.** No "10 reasons to build a deck." Specific, numbered, NoVA-data-backed posts only.
- **Stop trying to rank on Pinterest/Instagram for SEO.** They're useful for brand exposure, not ranking. Don't sink content time there.
- **Stop running paid ads to ldndecks.com homepage.** Run them to specific landing pages (city + service combos) to track funnel attribution properly.
- **Stop adding city pages where you don't actually serve.** Removes long-tail bloat AND legal claim risk.

## Final word

Most of this is **ship discipline, not strategic genius**. You're already the best-architected deck-builder site in NoVA. The gap to category dominance is:

1. Fix the 14 bugs (week 1)
2. Lock the schema moat (weeks 2–4)
