## What gets HARD in 90 days

The 5 honest challenges:

1. **Differentiating 26 city pages takes real research time.** It's not template work. Budget 2–3 hours per city to interview your project history and write unique copy. If overwhelmed, prioritize the 8 highest-revenue cities first; the rest can be uniformly filled in over months 4–6.

2. **Real estate agent outreach is slow.** Expect 1 partnership per 5 cold pitches. The win rate goes up after you've sent the 4th referral that closes for an agent. Year 2 is when partnerships compound.

3. **Sustained 8 blog posts/month is aggressive.** Consider hiring a NoVA-focused writer (NARI/NAHB-adjacent contractors, agencies like Hawke Media or content-mill alternatives like Compose.ly). Vet for industry knowledge — generic writers will produce generic posts.

4. **Review velocity beyond 5/month.** You'll need to formalize the post-project review-ask process: a project-completion email with a one-click review link AND a follow-up SMS at day 3 AND an in-person ask at the final walkthrough. Run this for 3 months; it should produce 5–10 reviews/month sustainably.

5. **Financing partner approval timelines.** Hearth applications can stretch 3 weeks; some require 1099 contractor review. Apply early. Have a backup partner if first chosen doesn't approve.

| Schema fixes break a page | Roll out in canary; smoke-test 5 critical pages before full deploy |
| New pages thin on launch | Each new page must hit minimum word counts in [05](05-ON-PAGE-TEMPLATES.md): service ≥1,500 words, city ≥1,800, comparison ≥1,500 |

| Media pitches ignored | Track open rates; refine subject lines; pivot to op-ed format |
| GBP review velocity stalls | Move from email-only to email+SMS+in-person triple-touch |
| Differentiation script breaks similarity test | Run script weekly; if any pair > 50%, re-write within 7 days |
