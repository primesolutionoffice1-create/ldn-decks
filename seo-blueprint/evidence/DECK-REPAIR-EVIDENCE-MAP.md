# Deck Repair Evidence Map

Created: 2026-06-01

This file maps owner evidence to the deck repair hub requirements without fabricating proof.

## Publish Rule

Only use `verified` records from `project-evidence-ledger.json` in public proof modules, captions, schema, testimonials, project cards, FAQs, or AI Overview-targeted passages.

`missing`, `partial`, and `blocked` records can guide internal work, but they must not become public proof claims.

## Photo Intake Requirements

- Hero before/after.
- Ledger failure.
- Post rot.
- Stair rebuild.
- Railing repair.
- Resurfacing before/after.
- Joist sistering or framing repair.
- Optional permit or inspection screenshot with private information removed.

## Cost Evidence Needed

Do not publish final repair-cost ranges until at least one of these source types exists in the ledger:

- Historical invoices.
- Accepted estimates.
- Calculator data tied to repair scopes.
- Owner-confirmed pricing policy.

Priority repair scopes:

- Joist sistering.
- Ledger reflash/rebolt.
- Post replacement.
- Emergency stabilization.

## Warranty Evidence Needed

Do not publish an exact repair warranty term until the ledger includes verified wording for:

- Workmanship duration.
- Structural repair limitations.
- Labor/material split.
- Resurfacing-specific coverage, if different.

## Review Evidence Needed

Individual review excerpts must be copied from a public profile or owner-supplied packet and recorded with:

- Source platform.
- Source URL.
- Date captured.
- Reviewer display name exactly as public.
- Excerpt text.
- Permission/status notes where applicable.
