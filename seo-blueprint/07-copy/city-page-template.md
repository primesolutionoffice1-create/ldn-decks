## Per-city differentiation checklist (REQUIRED to clear 64%-similarity issue)

For each city, fill in 5 unique fields:

| Field | Source |
|---|---|
| ARCHITECTURAL_NOTES | What's typical in this city — colonial/transitional/craftsman/modern, lot sizes |
| SUBDIVISIONS | 3 named subdivisions in this city |
| HOA_LIST | 3–7 HOAs you've worked with in this city |
| LANDMARKS | 1–2 named landmarks for AI Overview |
| PROJECT | 1 named recent project (anonymized first names ok) |
| CITY_SPECIFIC_COST_FACTOR | What makes this city more/less expensive (soil, slope, HOA strictness) |
| CITY_TIMELINE_DETAIL | What this city's timeline looks like (HOA response speed, county queue) |
| CITY_SPECIFIC_MATERIAL_NOTE | What materials owners in this city prefer |
| CITY_DESIGN_CONSIDERATION | Lot shape, sun exposure, view considerations |

When all 9 cities have these filled, pairwise similarity drops below 40%.

| Field | Ashburn | Leesburg |
|---|---|---|
| ARCHITECTURAL_NOTES | Transitional and craftsman; tight-grid subdivisions, 1/4–1/3 acre | Mix of colonial in historic district + new construction in River Creek; larger 1/3–1+ acre lots |
| SUBDIVISIONS | Brambleton, Belmont CC, Ashburn Village | River Creek, Tavistock Farms, Lansdowne on the Potomac |
| HOA_LIST | Brambleton, Belmont CC, Ashburn Village, Beacon Crest | River Creek, Tavistock, Lansdowne, Beacon Hill |
| LANDMARKS | One Loudoun, Loudoun Station, Ashburn Ice House | Leesburg Premium Outlets, downtown historic district, Potomac River |
| CITY_SPECIFIC_COST_FACTOR | Tight neighbor proximity often requires noise mitigation and tighter project staging | Sloped lots toward the Potomac often require multi-level designs and additional engineering |
| CITY_TIMELINE_DETAIL | Brambleton ARC typically returns in 1–2 weeks | River Creek ARC sometimes takes 3–4 weeks during peak season |
| CITY_SPECIFIC_MATERIAL_NOTE | Trex Transcend in Spiced Rum is the most-requested | Cedar and IPE remain popular in River Creek for the natural-wood aesthetic |
| CITY_DESIGN_CONSIDERATION | Many Brambleton homes face west; sun shading and fan integration matter | Many Leesburg homes have river views; deck sight-line preservation drives railing choice (cable rail) |
