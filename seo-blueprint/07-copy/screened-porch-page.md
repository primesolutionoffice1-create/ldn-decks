## H2 — "Why Northern Virginia Homeowners Add a Screened Porch"

> A screened porch is the highest-return outdoor improvement in NoVA. You get a usable outdoor room from late March through November — no pollen on the cushions in April, no mosquitoes in July, no leaf debris in October. It extends your home's living space without the cost or permitting of a four-season addition.
>
> Most of the screened porches we build are added onto an existing deck (composite or wood). When the existing frame is sound, we can preserve it and add the screen room above. When it isn't, we re-engineer the footing and ledger to support the roof load — a structural step many builders skip.

## H2 — "Screened Porch FAQs"

**Q: How much does a screened porch cost in Northern Virginia?**
A: Standard 12×16 screened porches typically run $20,000–$30,000 in NoVA, including roof, screen system, composite or wood floor, electrical rough-in, and ceiling fan. Vaulted-ceiling builds add 30–40%. Premium builds (16×20 with retractable screens, fireplace, custom finish) range $45,000–$70,000.

**Q: Can you add a screened porch to my existing deck?**
A: Yes — when the existing deck frame, ledger, and footings can support the additional roof load. We do a structural review during the consultation. About 70% of existing decks can support a screened porch without re-footing; the rest need supplemental support.

**Q: How long does a screened porch take to build?**
A: After permit approval, the build itself takes 3–5 weeks for a standard porch. Adding HOA approval (2–4 weeks) and permit (2–4 weeks), the full project from signed contract is 7–13 weeks.

**Q: What's the difference between a screened porch and a three-season room?**
A: A screened porch has mesh walls. A three-season room has glass walls (single-pane, sliding) plus screens. Three-season rooms are usable in colder shoulder seasons but cost 50–80% more. See the full breakdown: [three-season room vs screened porch vs sunroom](/blog/three-season-room-vs-screened-porch-vs-sunroom).

**Q: Do you offer retractable screens?**
A: Yes — Phantom retractable screen systems are available on most builds. They allow the porch to function as a screened porch in summer and an open porch in fall. Adds $3,000–$8,000 depending on opening size.

**Q: Can the screened porch have a ceiling fan and lighting?**
A: Yes — we include electrical rough-in for ceiling fans and recessed lighting on most builds. A licensed electrician handles the connections; the work is permitted and inspected.

**Q: Does a screened porch need a foundation?**
A: A screened porch built atop an existing deck uses the deck's footings. A new ground-up screened porch requires concrete footings sized for both the deck load and the roof load — typically 24–36" deep, depending on county frost depth (NoVA: 30" minimum).

**Q: Will my HOA approve a screened porch?**
A: Most NoVA HOAs approve screened porches that match the home's architectural style. We submit your drawings to the HOA Architectural Review Committee as part of every build. We've been approved through every major Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William, and Arlington HOA.
