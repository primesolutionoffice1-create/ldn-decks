## H2 — "Frequently Asked Questions"

(Single FAQPage block — fix the duplicate-FAQ issue from [01-AUDIT-FINDINGS.md §1](../01-AUDIT-FINDINGS.md).)

**Q: How long does a full deck build take in NoVA?**
A: From signed contract to final walkthrough: typically 6–10 weeks. The HOA + permit phase runs 2–4 weeks; the build itself runs 2–4 weeks for a typical residential deck. Larger or multi-level builds can stretch to 6 build-weeks.

**Q: What does "TrexPro Platinum" actually mean?**
A: Trex grants installer certifications in three tiers: TrexPro, TrexPro Gold, and TrexPro Platinum. Platinum is the highest tier — held by fewer than 2% of certified contractors nationwide. It requires a multi-year installation track record, training certification, and verified customer satisfaction.

**Q: Do I need a permit for a deck in Northern Virginia?**
A: Yes for any deck more than 30 inches off grade in Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William, Arlington, and Stafford counties. We handle the entire permit process — drawings, submission, inspections — as part of every build. See our county-by-county [permit guides](/deck-permit-loudoun-county-virginia).

**Q: What's the difference between composite, Trex, and wood decking?**
A: Trex is a brand of composite decking. "Composite" includes Trex, TimberTech, AZEK, and Deckorators — manufactured boards that resist rot, fading, and staining. "Wood" includes pressure-treated, cedar, IPE, and mahogany — traditional, refinishable, but requires staining every 2–3 years. See [composite vs wood comparison](/composite-deck-vs-wood-deck-virginia).

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Yes — we work with [partner name] for project financing with rates from {X}% APR and same-day pre-approval. Many homeowners finance the full deck for $200–$400/month. Use our [payment estimator](/deck-payment-estimator).

**Q: What's your warranty?**
A: 2-year workmanship warranty on all labor, layered on top of manufacturer warranties (Trex 25-year fade/stain, TimberTech 30-year, AZEK 50-year limited).

**Q: How quickly can you start?**
A: From signed contract: 4–8 weeks to build start, depending on the season. Spring and early summer book the fastest; September–November is the underrated sweet spot for fast starts.
