### Trex

The original composite and one of the brands homeowners ask about most often. Three core lines:

- **Trex Transcend** — flagship board, deep multi-color streaking, 25-year fade/stain warranty, premium feel. Best for entertainment-grade decks.
- **Trex Enhance Naturals** — mid-tier, lighter weight, lower price, 25-year warranty.
- **Trex Select** — entry-tier composite, narrower color range, 25-year warranty, best for budget-conscious projects.

[→ Trex deck installation details](/trex-decks) · [→ Trex Transcend in NoVA review](/trex-transcend-review-northern-virginia) · [→ Trex performance products](/trex-performance-products)

### TimberTech (AZEK Building Products)

PVC capped polymer (Vintage, Harvest) and capped composite (Edge, Reserve). 30-year fade/stain warranty on capped polymer. Very realistic wood grain in Vintage Mahogany.

### AZEK

100% capped polymer — no organic core. The longest manufacturer warranty in the category (50-year limited). Best heat performance in direct sun. Premium price tier.

### Deckorators

Mineral-based composite (Voyage line) — uniquely doesn't expand/contract with temperature like wood-based composites. Lower per-board cost than premium Trex. 50-year structural warranty.

## H2 — "Composite Deck FAQs"

**Q: How long does a composite deck last in Northern Virginia?**
A: Composite decking is engineered for long service life, commonly 25–50 years depending on the product line and warranty. NoVA's freeze-thaw cycles and humidity make correct installation, ventilation, cleaning, and drainage important. See [how long does a composite deck last](/how-long-does-a-composite-deck-last).

**Q: Trex vs TimberTech vs AZEK — which should I choose?**
A: Trex Transcend is the most popular for general use. TimberTech Vintage has the most realistic wood grain. AZEK Vintage offers the longest warranty and best heat performance. We bring samples of all three to your consultation. Full breakdown: [Trex vs TimberTech vs AZEK](/trex-vs-timbertech-vs-azek).

**Q: Is composite decking hot in summer?**
A: Composite reaches surface temps similar to wood and lighter colors stay 10–20°F cooler than darker. AZEK and Trex Transcend in lighter shades (Spiced Rum, Toasted Sand) measure 110–125°F in direct NoVA sun in July — comfortable barefoot for most. Darker shades (Lava Rock) reach 140°F+. We recommend material color planning for sun-exposed builds.

**Q: Can you install composite on my existing deck frame?**
A: Yes — if the frame passes a structural inspection. We do this regularly through our [deck resurfacing service](/services/deck-resurfacing). It saves 40–60% vs full replacement.

**Q: What's the cheapest composite decking?**
A: Trex Select runs $25–$40/sq ft installed in NoVA — the entry tier of branded composite. Cheaper "no-name" composite exists but voids most manufacturer warranty paths and tends to fade unevenly within 5–7 years. We don't install no-name composite.

**Q: Do you handle the HOA approval for a composite deck?**
A: Yes. We submit drawings to your HOA's Architectural Review Committee (or equivalent) as part of every build. We're approved by [Brambleton](/brambleton-hoa-deck-rules), [One Loudoun](/one-loudoun-hoa-deck-rules), [Lansdowne](/lansdowne-hoa-deck-rules), [Stone Ridge](/stone-ridge-hoa-deck-rules), and most NoVA HOAs.

**Q: How long does a composite deck build take?**
A: After permit approval (2–4 weeks), the build itself runs 2–4 weeks for a standard 400–600 sq ft deck. Larger or multi-level builds can take 5–6 build-weeks.

**Q: Do you offer financing for composite decks?**
A: Yes. Pre-approval in minutes through our financing partner. Most NoVA composite decks finance for $200–$500/month. [See payment estimator](/deck-payment-estimator).
