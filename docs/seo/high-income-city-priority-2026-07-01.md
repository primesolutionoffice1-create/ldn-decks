## Primary 5 For Immediate Work

Work first on:

1. McLean
2. Great Falls
3. Vienna
4. Oakton
5. Ashburn

Reason: this gives the best blend of high-ticket potential plus realistic lead volume. McLean and Great Falls are premium-ticket anchors. Vienna and Oakton expand the affluent Fairfax cluster. Ashburn supplies volume and strong HOA/composite replacement intent.

## Secondary 5

Then work:

1. Reston
2. Fairfax
3. Arlington
4. Alexandria
5. Leesburg

Reason: these matter, but they need tighter positioning. Arlington and Alexandria should not use generic suburban deck messaging; they need urban-lot, rooftop, historic-district, townhome, and permitting angles. Fairfax and Reston need stronger repair/replacement and permit-proof content. Leesburg needs more local project proof.

## What Each City Page Must Get Before More Indexing Or Ads Spend

Each priority city page needs:

- Real local project proof or clearly labeled owner-supplied project evidence.
- Neighborhood list tied to actual buyer intent, not filler.
- Permit / HOA section specific to the city or jurisdiction.
- One premium project scenario with realistic budget range.
- One repair/replacement scenario where relevant.
- One screened porch / outdoor living scenario where relevant.
- Strong CTA section using city name and service type.
- Internal links to the correct permit, HOA, cost, composite, screened porch, and estimate pages.
- No generic “deck builder near me” filler.
