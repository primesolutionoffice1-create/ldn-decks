## Page section template

Each priority city page should include:

- H1 with city + service.
- 120-180 word local intro, no generic filler.
- Services offered in that city.
- Local project proof: project type, material, neighborhood/city, problem solved.
- Permit/HOA expectations for that city/county.
- Composite vs wood recommendation for Virginia weather.
- Cost range and consultation CTA.
- 5 natural FAQs.
- Internal links to county page, service page, cost guide, and estimate page.

## Keep/consolidate rule

If a location page cannot be made meaningfully unique with local proof, keep it out of the priority indexing queue or consolidate it into a stronger county/service page.
