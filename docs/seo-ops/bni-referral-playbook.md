# BNI Referral Playbook for LDN Decks

## Purpose

BNI should be treated as a referral channel, not as a direct SEO channel. The goal is to meet local professionals who already talk to homeowners before deck, porch, patio, and outdoor living projects begin.

## Best Chapter Fit

Score each chapter from 1 to 5 before joining.

| Signal | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Category fit | Deck builder, outdoor living, remodeling, or contractor seat is open |
| Homeowner network | Realtors, home inspectors, mortgage brokers, insurance agents, designers, landscapers, remodelers, roofers, siding/window contractors |
| Local coverage | Strong presence in Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William, Arlington, or Stafford |
| Referral quality | Members understand $15k-$75k residential projects, not only small handyman jobs |
| Attendance quality | Members show up consistently and can explain their ideal referral clearly |

Do not join only because the chapter has open seats. Join only if the room can produce homeowner introductions that match LDN Decks projects.

## Ideal Referral Ask

The best referral is a homeowner in Northern Virginia who is considering one of these projects:

- Replacing an aging wood deck with composite decking
- Building a new custom deck
- Adding a screened porch, pergola, patio, or outdoor living space
- Preparing a home for sale and needing deck repair, resurfacing, or inspection
- Buying a home with an unsafe, old, or poorly built deck
- Navigating HOA, permit, or material decisions before starting a project

## 30-Second Pitch

LDN Decks builds and replaces decks, screened porches, patios, pergolas, and outdoor living spaces across Northern Virginia. A great referral for us is a homeowner with an aging deck or a family planning a larger backyard upgrade in Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William, Arlington, or Stafford. We handle design guidance, materials, permits, and clear estimates so your client knows exactly what comes next.

## 60-Second Pitch

Most homeowners do not know whether they need a full deck replacement, resurfacing, structural repair, or a new design. LDN Decks helps them figure that out before they commit to a contractor. We specialize in composite decks, Trex and TimberTech projects, screened porches, patios, pergolas, railings, and full outdoor living spaces across Northern Virginia. A strong referral is a homeowner with an older wood deck, a buyer or seller dealing with an inspection concern, or a family planning a backyard project in the next 6 months. Send us the name, phone number, city, project type, and timeline. We will respond professionally, protect your relationship, and give them clear next steps.

## Best Referral Partners

- Realtors: sellers preparing homes, buyers finding unsafe decks, relocation clients
- Home inspectors: inspection issues, structural concerns, aging deck warnings
- Mortgage and insurance professionals: renovation conversations, claim-related exterior work
- Architects and designers: outdoor living layouts, porch/deck/patio planning
- Landscapers and hardscape contractors: projects that need decks, stairs, railings, or porches
- Remodelers, roofers, siding, and window contractors: adjacent exterior projects
- Property managers and HOA contacts: community rules, compliance, replacement needs

## Visit Checklist

Before visiting:

- Confirm the deck builder/outdoor living/remodeling category is open
- Review member roster for the partner types above
- Prepare the 30-second pitch
- Bring business cards and the referral partner URL: https://ldndecks.com/referral-partners

During the visit:

- Ask who in the room works with homeowners before renovations
- Listen for members who mention inspections, listings, landscaping, insurance, or design
- Ask whether they commonly see aging decks or outdoor living requests
- Do not hard sell. Set one-to-one meetings with the strongest partners

After the visit:

- Follow up within 24 hours with the partner page
- Schedule one-to-one calls with 3-5 high-fit members
- Track chapter name, member names, category, possible referrals, and next action

## One-to-One Questions

- What type of client is best for you?
- When do you usually hear about deck, porch, patio, or backyard projects?
- What problems do homeowners mention before they call a contractor?
- Do your clients ask for licensed/insured contractors or permit help?
- What would make LDN Decks easy to refer?
- Who would be a bad referral so we can avoid wasting time?

## 30/60/90 Day Test

First 30 days:

- Visit 2-3 Northern Virginia chapters
- Score each chapter using the fit table
- Book at least 6 one-to-one meetings
- Track all referral conversations in one sheet or CRM

Days 31-60:

- Choose the best chapter only if fit is clear
- Build a weekly referral ask around one project type
- Give referrals where legitimate
- Follow up with every possible lead within 24 hours

Days 61-90:

- Measure introductions, estimates booked, qualified opportunities, and closed revenue
- Keep the chapter only if there is visible referral momentum
- Improve pitch based on the questions members actually ask

## ROI Gate

BNI is worth testing if it can produce at least one qualified $20k+ closed project per year or a steady stream of warm estimate requests from trusted local professionals. If the chapter mostly generates small repair asks, low-budget handyman work, or weak networking activity, do not keep investing time there.
