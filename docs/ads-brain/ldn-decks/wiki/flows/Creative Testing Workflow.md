# Creative Testing Workflow

## Purpose

Track creative concepts, asset fatigue, format coverage, platform-native
constraints, and testing cadence.

## Minimum Evidence

- Creative or ad export.
- Spend or impressions.
- CTR, CVR, CPA, ROAS, or qualified lead quality when available.
- Launch date or first-seen date.

## Output

Update [[Creative Fatigue Board]] and create experiment notes when a new test is
approved.
