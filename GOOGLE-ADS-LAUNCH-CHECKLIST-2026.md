# Google Ads Launch Checklist 2026

Use this before enabling spend.

## 1. Conversion Setup

- Create or confirm `Qualified Call (Ads) - 60s`.
- Set as Primary.
- Count: One.
- Include in Conversions: On.
- Verify call asset uses the same conversion action.
- Keep form submits as Secondary until lead quality is confirmed.
- Keep calls under 60 seconds out of optimization.

## 2. Location Setup

- Target Loudoun County, Fairfax County, and Prince William County.
- Location option: Presence only.
- Do not use "Presence or interest".
- Do not add city targets at launch.
- Do not broaden beyond the 3 counties during learning.

## 3. Campaigns

- `SRCH | Composite | 3 Counties | Calls` - $80/day.
- `SRCH | Replacement + Resurfacing | 3 Counties | Calls` - $50/day.
- `SRCH | Branded | 3 Counties | Calls` - $10/day.
- `RMKT | PMax | Visitors 30/60/90d | Calls` - $10/day.

## 4. Search Campaign QA

- Use Exact and Phrase only.
- No Broad match at launch.
- Add the shared negative keyword list before launch.
- Keep `Composite Deck Cost` in its own ad group.
- Keep branded campaign separate.
- Use at least 2 RSAs per ad group.
- Pin only if legally or strategically necessary; otherwise let RSA rotate.
- Confirm all RSA headlines are 30 characters or less.
- Confirm all RSA descriptions are 90 characters or less.

## 5. Landing Page QA

- Composite Search final URL: `/composite-decks/`.
- Replacement Search final URL: `/services/deck-replacement/`.
- Resurfacing education URL: `/deck-resurfacing-vs-replacement/`.
- Branded final URL: `/contact/` or homepage.
- Confirm `/deck-replacement` redirects to `/services/deck-replacement`.
- Confirm pages mention projects from $15k+ or realistic replacement pricing.
- Confirm pages mention permits and HOA.
- Confirm pages have call-first CTA.

## 7. PMax Remarketing Guardrails

- Use remarketing only.
- Audience signals: visitors 30, 60, 90 days.
- Final URL expansion: Off.
- Do not allow PMax to send traffic to low-intent blog URLs.
- Optimize only toward `Qualified Call (Ads) - 60s`.

## 8. First 7 Days

- Bid strategy: Maximize Conversions, no tCPA.
- Check search terms 2-3 times per week.
- Add negatives aggressively.
- Do not increase budgets during early learning.
- Do not add broad match.
- Watch calls for quality, not just volume.

## 9. tCPA Gate

Only consider tCPA after 15-30 qualified conversions per campaign.

- Composite: $130-$160.
- Replacement: $120-$150.
- Branded: usually no tCPA initially.

## 10. Weekly Review

- Search terms: add negatives.
- Converting phrase terms: move to exact.
- Keywords with spend and no qualified calls: reduce or pause.
- Auction Insights: watch large lead aggregators and BuildHD-style competitors.
- Call recordings or call details: verify project size, county, service type, and budget fit.
