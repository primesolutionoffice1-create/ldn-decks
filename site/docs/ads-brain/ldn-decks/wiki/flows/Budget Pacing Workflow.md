# Budget Pacing Workflow

## Purpose

Translate spend, budget, CPA, ROAS, and conversion volume into safe pacing
recommendations.

## Guardrails

- Do not scale during active learning without explicit reason.
- Respect platform budget sufficiency requirements.
- Identify campaigns above 3x target CPA.
- Separate tracking problems from performance problems.
