## URL Structure Standards (codify these)

**Already aligned. No changes recommended for existing URLs.** Pattern:
- City pages: `/deck-builder-{city}-va` ✓
- County hubs: `/near-you/{county}` and one canonical `/deck-builders-loudoun` ✓
- Service pages: `/services/{service-slug}` and `/services/porches/{type}` ✓
- Comparison pages: `/{a}-vs-{b}-{geo?}` ✓
- Cost pages: `/{topic}-cost-{geo}` and `/{topic}-cost-calculator` ✓
- Permit pages: `/deck-permit-{jurisdiction}-virginia` ✓
- HOA pages: `/{community-or-county}-hoa-deck-rules` ✓
- Blog: `/blog/{slug}` ✓
- Showcase: `/showcase/{slug}` ✓

**One small refinement:** new HOA pages should follow `/{hoa-slug}-hoa-deck-rules` (no county prefix) to mirror the existing `/loudoun-county-hoa-deck-rules` pattern.

## Why this architecture works

1. **Topical depth without bloat.** You add 26 pages, kill 2, sunset 1. Net +23 indexable pages = 173 total. Still way under "thin content" risk.
2. **Pillar reinforcement.** Every new page reinforces an existing pillar; no new orphan silos.
3. **Local moat.** 6 NEW HOA pages + 3 NEW permit pages = 9 net-new local-pack signals. **No competitor has a single HOA page.**
4. **Defensive coverage.** Annandale (Fortress's HQ), Broadlands/Lansdowne (Loudoun affluence), Arlington/Stafford permits (Battlefield's coverage area).
5. **Cost-page county split.** 4 new county-specific cost pages let you rank in the long-tail of "deck cost {county}" — likely a high-converting query bucket.
6. **Financing transparency.** `/deck-payment-estimator` is the single highest-ROI new page if paired with a real partner.
