# Property Content with Existing Authority Audit

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Site-level quality signals now roll up — thin pages drag strong pages down.
> See **[[2026-december-core-update|December 2025 Core Update + 2026 E-E-A-T Reality]]** for current state.

*⚠️ Complete the* [Click Through Rate (CTR) Analysis](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=be54d5488bee48c59022f84a0caab827) *first and export your internal\_all file from Screaming Frog.*

Google recognizes domains as authorities on specific topics. When you publish new content within your established expertise areas, it inherits your existing authority and ranks faster – no waiting months for aging or heavy backlink investment.

Example: If Google trusts you on "vintage car restoration," new content about "classic car paint techniques" will rank quickly. This is topical relevance in action.

Find Content Opportunities with ChatGPT

1. Export your existing content data: URLs, title tags, meta descriptions. This is in your internal\_all file from Screaming Frog.  
2. Upload to ChatGPT with this [[property-content-with-authority-audit-prompt|prompt]]:  
3. Review suggestions for topics that relate to your best content and fill obvious gaps

The "Almost Winners" Strategy

Maximize what you already have before creating something new. This approach delivers the fastest results.

Focus on pages ranking in positions 11-30 (pages 2-3).

These pages are golden because Google already trusts them, and small improvements to these pages can 10x traffic. Plus, they're proven to already have some relevance.

Step 1: Find Your Targets

Using Ahrefs, Semrush, or Search Console:

* Filter for positions 11-30  
* Sort by search volume  
* Look for reasonable difficulty scores

Step 2: Evaluate Each Opportunity

Check:

* Current traffic (low \= safer to optimize)  
* Keyword difficulty (lower \= easier wins)  
* Search volume (higher \= more potential)  
* Intent alignment (can your page satisfy searchers?)

Step 3: Optimization Plan

1. Expand content depth with AI  
2. Optimize for target keywords (use Page Optimizer Pro)  
3. Match top 3 competitors' approach  
4. Build 1-2 quality backlinks

Quick Implementation

Create a simple spreadsheet:

* Target URL  
* Current position  
* Target keyword  
* Current vs. potential traffic  
* Priority level

This becomes your roadmap for quick wins in the first 60 days.

❌❌❌ Don't:

* Over-optimize pages already getting traffic  
* Ignore search intent  
* Manually request re-crawls  
* Sacrifice readability for keywords

✅✅✅ Do:

* Focus on truly underperforming pages  
* Always improve content quality  
* Let backlinks trigger natural re-crawls
