# E-E-a-T with “about Us” Page

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Specific E-E-A-T elements (named staff, certifications, real photos) are now load-bearing.
> See **[[2026-december-core-update|December 2025 Core Update + 2026 E-E-A-T Reality]]** for current state.

Your About page is critical for establishing E-E-A-T with Google. The days of using AI-generated personas with fake backstories are over—Google now requires proof that real people create your content.

The Six Essential Elements

1\. Contact Information

Be transparent and accessible. Include multiple ways to reach you—email, phone, contact form, and physical address if applicable. Google values businesses that don't hide behind anonymity.

2\. Personal Story

Share your genuine journey and expertise. Make it authentic with real experiences, verifiable through other online sources, and directly relevant to why you're qualified to serve your audience.

📌 **Related Learning**: Review [[what-is-e-e-a-t|What is E-E-A-T?]] to understand why author credibility matters for SEO.

3\. Author Bio Box

Create a professional author section with your headshot, credentials, and brief professional summary. Use this same format across all content pages to reinforce your authorship.

4\. Social Media Integration

Link to your active social profiles—LinkedIn, Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, and any industry-specific platforms. These profiles should use photos of the same person (but not identical images), maintain reasonable activity, and have complete information.

⚠️ **Important**: Inactive or incomplete social profiles can hurt more than help. Only link to profiles you actively maintain.

5\. Multimedia Proof

Video is your best option—record a brief introduction discussing your expertise and mission. If video isn't possible, use multiple photographs from different angles and contexts. A single headshot everywhere screams "fake" to Google's algorithms.

6\. Google Business Profile

Even non-local businesses benefit from a Google Business Profile. Local businesses should link their GBP to their homepage, while non-local businesses should link to their About page. This additional verification signal strengthens your legitimacy.

🔗 **Next Steps**: Learn more in our [Google Business Profile](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=7a06ff4df2bb44f28f8f2b2aeba3a1a7) module about optimizing your GBP for maximum impact.

Why This Matters Now

Five years ago, fake personas worked. Today, Google's algorithms detect and devalue sites lacking authentic human authorship. Your About page serves as the primary verification point—it's not just for Google, but often one of your most visited pages.

Remember to write genuinely, highlight specific achievements, and maintain consistency across all platforms. Your About page builds trust with both search engines and human visitors, making it worth the investment to get right.

🎯 **Action Item**: After creating your About page, use it as the foundation for consistent author information across all platforms.
