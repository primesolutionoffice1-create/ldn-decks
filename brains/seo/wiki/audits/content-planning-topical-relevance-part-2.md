# Content Planning: Topical Relevance Part 2

How to leverage AI to generate multiple keyword variations for your content topics? This technique helps you build comprehensive topical authority by creating semantically relevant content that Google recognizes as authoritative within your niche.

When you're building content at scale, keyword variations are essential. By creating multiple angles and approaches to the same topic, you:

* **Increase topical coverage** across your domain  
* **Build semantic relevance** that search engines recognize  
* **Create more opportunities** to rank for related searches  
* **Establish domain authority** in your chosen niche

The Process: From Topics to Keywords

Step 1: Start with Your Core Topics

Using the 25 relevant topics you identified in the previous lesson (see: [[content-planning-topical-relevance-part-1|**Generate Topical Relevance**]]), you'll now expand each one into multiple keyword variations.

Step 2: Generate Variations Using AI

For each of your 25 topics, you'll use Claude to generate:

* 15 keyword variations  
* SEO-optimized titles  
* Content headings  
* Basic content outlines

**ℹ️ Example:** If your topic is "Introduction to Graphic Design," the AI will generate 15 different keyword approaches to this topic, each with its own unique angle and search intent.

Step 3: Scale Your Content Strategy

Here's the math that makes this powerful:

* 25 core topics × 15 variations each \= **375 unique article ideas**  
* Each article targets a specific keyword variation  
* All articles remain semantically connected to your main niche

Domain Rating Considerations

Before implementing this strategy, consider your domain's current authority:

* **Higher DR sites** can target more competitive variations  
* **Newer sites** should focus on long-tail, less competitive variations  
* Adjust your keyword difficulty targets based on your site's strength

**❗ Important:** The examples in this lesson use graphic design as a niche. You must adapt all prompts and strategies to your specific industry or topic area.

The Content Creation Pipeline

Once you have your 375 article ideas complete with:

* Target keywords  
* SEO-optimized titles  
* Content headings  
* Basic outlines

You'll move to the next phase: using AI to write all 375 articles. This systematic approach allows you to build authority much faster than traditional content creation methods.
