# Content Planning: Geographical Relevance Part 2

After establishing your [Core 30 content structure](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=e64641a74ba146208b675d47f569c09b), the next challenge is determining which specific geographic areas and keywords to target first. Rather than guessing or randomly selecting neighborhoods, we'll use AI to analyze ranking data and prioritize content creation based on actual opportunity.

When working with local SEO, you'll often face hundreds or thousands of potential keyword-location combinations. For example, a plumbing company in a major metropolitan area might have:

* 50+ services to target  
* 20+ neighborhoods or suburbs  
* Multiple keyword variations for each service

This creates an overwhelming matrix of content possibilities. AI helps us cut through this complexity by analyzing ranking data and identifying the highest-impact opportunities first.

Working with Leadsnap Data

Step 1: Export Your Ranking Data

Start by accessing your Local Dominator dashboard and downloading the CSV export of your rank tracking data. This file contains comprehensive ranking information across all tracked keywords and locations.

📌 **Note:** The exported CSV typically contains 1,000+ rows of data, including rankings for your client and all tracked competitors. This is too large to process directly in Claude, so we'll need to filter it first.

Step 2: Filter for Your Client's Data

1. Open the CSV in your spreadsheet application  
2. Select the header row  
3. Apply a filter to the entire dataset  
4. In the "Business" column, unselect all options  
5. Select only your client's business name

Step 3: Prepare Data for Claude

Here's the crucial step that ensures Claude can process your data effectively:

1. Click on the column header to select the entire filtered view  
2. Navigate to **Home → Find & Select → Go to Special**  
3. Choose **Visible Cells Only**  
4. Copy the selected data

⚠️ **Important:** If you skip the "Visible Cells Only" step, you'll copy hidden rows and Claude will likely return an error about too much data.

Using Claude for Prioritization

Once you've prepared your filtered data, paste it into Claude along with our prioritization prompt. Claude will analyze:

Geographic Factors

* **Population density** of different neighborhoods  
* **Business concentration** in each area  
* **Competition levels** across locations  
* **Current ranking positions** by geography

Keyword Opportunities

* **Ranking gaps** where you're close to page one  
* **Search volume estimates** based on service type  
* **Competition difficulty** for specific terms  
* **Quick win opportunities** (rankings 4-10)  
* **Highly profitable areas** (current rankings lower, but demographics show high value ranking in this location)
