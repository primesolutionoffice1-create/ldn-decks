# GBP Services Prompt

> AI prompt extracted from [[gbp-services|GBP Services]]. Use as a reusable building block. Test with your own client's facts before shipping output.

## Prompt

You are a local SEO expert. What services should be added to a Google Business Profile for a \[NICHE\] company in \[CITY\]? Please be specific and list at least 20 services but feel free to list additional ones. Each service should be specific offerings and not broad categories. Also, include variations of services that might have different names but are commonly searched for by my target customers.

---

Quality Control: The Critical Step

Before adding any AI-generated services to your GBP, you must verify each one with your client. This verification process is non-negotiable for maintaining trust and avoiding customer disappointment.

⚠️ Cautionary Tale: We once added "solar water heater installation" to a plumber's service list without verification. The plumber received a call from a customer specifically requesting this service—which they didn't actually offer. This created an awkward situation and potentially lost business.

Your carefully curated service list serves a dual purpose. Not only does it enhance your GBP's visibility, but it also provides the foundation for selecting additional categories in the next phase of optimization.

⏩⏩ Related Lesson: After completing your services, you'll use this information in the [[gbp-categories|GBP Categories]] lesson to expand your category selections strategically.
