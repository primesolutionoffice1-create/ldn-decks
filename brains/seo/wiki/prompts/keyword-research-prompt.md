# Keyword research prompt

> AI prompt extracted from [[keyword-research|Keyword Research]]. Use as a reusable building block. Test with your own client's facts before shipping output.

## Prompt

You are a local search engine optimization expert. Give me some keyword ideas for a \[local Niche\] in \[City, State\]. Include keywords that target specific neighborhoods in this city and keywords for a variety of services a local \[Niche\] would want customers for. Keep in mind seasonal trends and local language nuances. Mention local events or landmarks in the keywords.
