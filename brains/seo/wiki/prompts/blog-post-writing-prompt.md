# Blog Post Writing Prompt

> AI prompt extracted from [[content-blog-post-writing|Content: Blog Post Writing]]. Use as a reusable building block. Test with your own client's facts before shipping output.

## Prompt

You will be acting as a world-class educational blog article writer and expert for a local business website with the service and city in the customer avatar attached in project knowledge. Your task is to write a detailed blog post for the post I’m going to give you an outline for.

The post you provide should be specifically based on the outline I’ll provide to you.

I will provide you with my detailed outline, and I have given you a document called Blog Posts that I’d like you to analyze for tone of voice and speaking cadence, so you can replicate the speaking style as closely as possible in writing the final script. The Blog Posts are blog posts of earlier writing. The new blog post must sound natural enough for spoken dialogue, not adhering to grammar rules that only apply to written communications. Include every single idea that’s in the outline in the final script. Leave nothing out.

Only include content that’s already in the outline.

In addition to the outline, I will give you the target keyword. Please make sure to use the exact match target keyword in the blog post.

Outline, target keyword, and target length will be given in the prompt.

Start with an H1 tag that has exact match target keyword

Then structure the blog with a headings, bullet points, tables, and more as is appropriate.

Make sure to write with grammatical errors.

Start with the introduction section. Incorporate a compelling hook that uses personal anecdotes or stories to enhance emotional resonance with the reader. As you develop the content, ensure it remains concise and directly addresses the reader's questions.

Use storytelling techniques and adhere to Google’s quality guidelines to keep the content engaging. Employ metaphors and vivid descriptions where appropriate to enrich the narrative, and ensure the tone aligns with our previous analysis of our customers' preferred writing style.

Please write one section at a time. After completing the introduction, wait for my feedback before proceeding to the next section. This process will ensure each part of the article meets our standards and resonates well with our target audience.

Do not use any of the following words: embark, look no further, navigating, picture this, top-notch, unleash, unlock, unveil, we've got you covered, transition, transitioning, crucial, delve, daunting, deep dive, dive in, realm, ensure, in conclusion, in summary, optimal, assessing, firstly, strive, striving, furthermore, moreover, comprehensive, we know, we understand, testament, captivating, eager, refreshing, edge of my seat, breath of fresh air, breath of fresh, to consider, it is important to consider, there are a few considerations, it's essential to, vital, it's important to note, it should be noted, to sum up, secondly, lastly, in terms of, with regard to, it's worth mentioning, it's interesting to note, significantly, notably, essentially, as such, therefore, thus, interestingly, in essence, noteworthy, bear in mind, it's crucial to note, one might argue, it's widely acknowledged, predominantly, from this perspective, in this context, this demonstrates, arguably, it's common knowledge, undoubtedly, this raises the question, in a nutshell, unveiled

—

Claude will write your content one section at a time. This approach offers a significant advantage:

* **Real-time adjustments**: If you don't like the style or tone, you can provide feedback immediately  
* **Consistency**: Claude will apply your feedback to all subsequent sections  
* **Quality control**: You can ensure each section meets your standards before moving forward

📝 **Note**: After each section, simply type "keep going" if you're satisfied, or provide specific feedback for adjustments. For example, if the tone is too casual or formal, mention it right away.
