# Backlink competition prompt

> AI prompt extracted from [[backlinking-competitive-research-to-find-opportunities|Backlinking : Competitive Research to Find Opportunities]]. Use as a reusable building block. Test with your own client's facts before shipping output.

## Prompt

Here is a csv of referring domains to my organic competitor. I've also included a csv of referring domains to my site, **\[domain\]**. Please analyze these two csv files and let me know which referring domains my competitor has links from that I do not. Focus on domains that would be relatively straightforward to get links to my site based on how the site operates, such as paid post placement, paid link insertions, sponsorship opportunities, post or blog commenting, and forum posting. 

You can also search for opportunities to comment on blogs:

2. `"Post a comment"`  
3. `"Add a comment"`  
4. `"Submit a comment"`  
5. `"Write a comment"`  
6. `"Sign in to comment"`  
7. `"Discussion"`  
8. `"Join the discussion"`  
9. `"Reply to comment"`  
10. `"Comments powered by Disqus"`  
11. `"Be the first to comment"`  
12. `"Leave a reply"`  
13. `"Your email address will not be published"`  
14. `"Notify me of follow-up comments"`  
15. `"Comment on this post"`

Combine them with keywords related to your niche with examples below:

* `"Leave a comment" "SEO blog"`  
* `"Post a comment" "digital marketing"`  
* `"Add a comment" "content marketing"`
