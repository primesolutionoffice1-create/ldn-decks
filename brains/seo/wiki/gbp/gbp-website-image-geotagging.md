# GBP + Website Image Geotagging

Geotagging adds geographical location data to your images' metadata, helping search engines understand where your business operates. 

* We currently use the GeoTag app on Mac.

Does It Work?

While some SEOs debate its effectiveness, we've seen clients experience overnight ranking improvements after uploading geotagged images.

💡 Our Philosophy: If it takes less than 30 minutes, just do it. The potential upside outweighs the minimal time investment.

The Google Business Profile Question

Yes, Google strips metadata from images uploaded to GBP. However, this doesn't mean Google ignores the data during upload. They could process the location information before removing it.

Quick Process

1. Upload images to GeoTag  
2. Enter the business location  
3. Click "Geotag"  
4. Upload to your website and GBP
