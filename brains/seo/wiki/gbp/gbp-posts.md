# GBP Posts

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Cadence raised: minimum 2× per week in 2026; scheduling is now native.
> See **[[2026-gbp-changes|2026 GBP Changes]]** for current state.

🤖 Basic Prompt 🤖

Create 52 engaging Google Business Profile posts for **\[BUSINESS NAME\]**, a **\[BUSINESS TYPE\]** located in **\[CITY/REGION\]**. Each post should be between 150-300 characters, include a clear call-to-action, and be designed to increase engagement, visibility, and conversions.

Do not include email address, phone number, or website url in the GBP posts.

* Business name: **\[INSERT\]**  
* Business type/industry: **\[INSERT\]**  
* Location: **\[INSERT\]**  
* Primary services/products: **\[LIST 3-5 MAIN OFFERINGS\]**  
* Unique selling propositions: **\[LIST 2-3 THINGS THAT MAKE YOUR BUSINESS SPECIAL\]**  
* Target audience: **\[[creating-an-audience-avatar|Ideal Audience, [Audience Avatar]]\]**  
* Business hours: **\[INSERT\]**  
* Website URL: **\[INSERT\]**  
* Contact information: **\[PHONE, EMAIL\]**  
* **Promotional Posts** (13 posts)  
  1. Special offers and discounts  
  2. New products/services  
  3. Limited-time promotions  
  4. Loyalty programs  
* **Educational Posts** (13 posts)  
  1. Industry tips and advice  
  2. How-to content related to your products/services  
  3. Frequently asked questions  
  4. Myth-busting about your industry  
* **Engagement Posts** (13 posts)  
  1. Customer testimonials and success stories  
  2. Behind-the-scenes content  
  3. Team spotlights  
  4. Community involvement  
* **Seasonal/Event Posts** (13 posts)  
  1. Holiday-related content  
  2. Local events participation  
  3. Industry-specific seasonal trends  
  4. Business milestones and anniversaries  
* For each post, include:  
  1. An attention-grabbing headline (when applicable)  
  2. Concise, value-driven body text (150-300 characters)  
  3. A clear call-to-action  
  4. A recommendation for the type of image to include  
  5. Relevant hashtags (2-3 maximum)  
* **Calls-to-Action**: Vary between "Call now," "Book online," "Learn more," "Visit our website," "Stop by today," etc.  
* **Time Sensitivity**: Include phrases like "Limited time," "This week only," "While supplies last" for promotional posts  
* **Keywords**: Incorporate location-specific and industry-specific keywords naturally  
* **Formatting**: Use emoji sparingly but strategically to draw attention to key points  
* **January-March**: Focus on new year goals, winter services/products, early spring preparation  
* **April-June**: Highlight spring offerings, Mother's/Father's Day, graduation season, summer preparation  
* **July-September**: Emphasize summer services, back-to-school transitions, early fall offerings  
* **October-December**: Feature fall services, holiday preparation, end-of-year promotions, gift ideas

Example Post Format

**\[POST CATEGORY: Promotional\]**

**Image: \[Product collection with sale graphic overlay\]**

**Headline:** Summer Special: 20% Off All **\[Product Category\]**\!

**Body:** Beat the heat with our biggest discount of the season\! All **\[specific products\]** are 20% off through June. Perfect for **\[specific benefit to customer\]**.

**CTA:** Shop online or visit our store today before this deal melts away\!
