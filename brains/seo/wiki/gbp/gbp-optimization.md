# GBP Optimization

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Algorithm shifted to engagement-weighted; 30-day inactivity decay is steep.
> See **[[2026-gbp-changes|2026 GBP Changes]]** for current state.

When optimizing a Google Business Profile, completeness matters. Every empty field is a missed opportunity to provide valuable information to potential customers and strengthen your local SEO presence. This lesson covers the essential practice of ensuring every available field in your GBP is properly filled out.

⚠️ It's much easier and faster to rank GBPs with addresses listed, so if possible, try to list the business' address, even if the client does not have a physical location yet.

A fully completed Google Business Profile serves multiple purposes:

* Provides comprehensive information to potential customers  
* Signals to Google that the business is active and legitimate  
* Improves visibility in local search results  
* Increases trust and credibility with users

Key Areas to Complete

Social Profiles

One of the most commonly overlooked sections is social media profiles. An incomplete social profile section sends the wrong signal about your business's online presence.

What to include:

* Facebook page URL  
* Twitter/X profile  
* LinkedIn company page  
* Instagram profile  
* YouTube channel (if applicable)  
* Any other relevant social platforms

💡 Pro Tip: Even if a business isn't highly active on all platforms, having the profiles linked shows legitimacy and provides multiple ways for customers to connect.

Operating Hours

Beyond basic hours, ensure you've addressed:

* Regular business hours for each day of the week  
* Special hours for holidays and events  
* More hours section if the business has different hours for specific services

⏩⏩ Related Lesson: See our lesson on [[gbp-holiday-hours-maintenance-automated-calls|*Upcoming Holiday Hours*]] for detailed guidance on managing seasonal schedule changes.

Additional Information Fields

Work through every available option in the edit profile section:

Accessibility Features

* Wheelchair accessible entrance  
* Wheelchair accessible parking  
* Wheelchair accessible restroom  
* Other accessibility accommodations

Service Options

* Online appointments  
* On-site services  
* Delivery options  
* Curbside pickup

Amenities

* Wi-Fi availability  
* Parking options  
* Payment methods accepted  
* Health and safety measures

The Systematic Approach

To ensure nothing is missed, follow this process:

1. Access the edit profile section of your Google Business Profile  
2. Review each category systematically from top to bottom  
3. Fill in every applicable field \- if it applies to the business, it should be completed  
4. Double-check for completeness before saving

⚠️ Important: Don't skip fields just because they seem minor. Google uses all available data to understand and rank your business.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

* Leaving fields blank when the information is available  
* Assuming some fields don't matter \- they all contribute to your profile's completeness  
* Forgetting to update when business details change  
* Not exploring all available options in each section

Be Thorough

Take the time to explore every section and subsection. New fields and options are occasionally added by Google, so periodic reviews are beneficial.

Be Accurate

Ensure all information is current and correct. Inaccurate information can harm your credibility and confuse potential customers.

Be Consistent

Information across all fields should align with what's on your website and other online profiles.

📊 Quick Check: After completing all fields, your GBP should show 100% profile completion in the dashboard.
