# December 2025 Core Update + 2026 E-E-A-T Reality

> **Why this note exists:** Google ran the **December 2025 Core Update**
> (Dec 11 → Dec 29, 2025) followed by the **March 2026 Core Update**. Both
> materially changed how E-E-A-T is enforced. The seeded chapters were
> written pre-update.

## The single biggest change

**E-E-A-T now applies to ALL competitive queries, not just YMYL.**

Pre-December-2025:

- E-E-A-T was *primarily* a health, finance, legal (YMYL) signal
- Lifestyle / entertainment / hobby sites could rank without strong
  expertise signals

Post-December-2025:

- Even **entertainment, lifestyle, and product-review** content needs
  demonstrated expertise to rank
- Generic AI-generated content gets filtered more aggressively
- "I am an SEO content writer" no longer cuts it — you need topical
  expertise, not writing expertise

For Local SEO, this is mostly *additive* — local always had E-E-A-T
pressure via map pack + reviews. But for affiliate / lifestyle clients,
the shift is significant.

## What the December 2025 winners looked like

Sites that gained visibility in the Dec 2025 update shared these traits:

| Trait | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Detailed service descriptions from named, experienced staff** | "Written by Jane Smith, 15 years master plumber" — not "Our team has decades of experience" |
| **Community involvement** | Real local sponsorships, school partnerships, chamber memberships — documented with photos |
| **Staff profiles with experience** | Bio pages with credentials, certifications, years of experience |
| **Original photos** | Of work, facilities, team. Not stock photos. |
| **Client testimonials with specifics** | "Replaced our 1998 Lennox furnace with an XYZ model in November 2024" — not "Great service!" |
| **Behavioral signals** | Lower bounce, longer dwell, return visits matter more |

A documented winning local example: 487 Google reviews (4.8 avg), detailed
service-area pages with **local climate considerations**, owner's 25-year
expertise highlighted on About page.

## What lost in December 2025

- "SEO-optimized" service pages without substantive value
- Thin city/area pages with only NAP and a few generic paragraphs
- Generic AI-generated content with no expert review
- Stock-photo-heavy pages
- Pages with no author attribution

## March 2026 Core Update

Built on December 2025. Key emphasis:

- **Behavioral signals carry more weight** (bounce, dwell, return)
- **Site-level quality signals roll up to URL-level rankings** — a few
  thin pages can drag down strong pages
- **Forum / community content** got a boost (see [[2026-reddit-llm-citation-engine|Reddit LLM Citation Engine]])

## What this changes about the seeded playbook

### `what-is-e-e-a-t` chapter

- Still strategically correct — the four pillars are unchanged.
- **Add:** "Applies to all competitive queries now, not just YMYL."
- **Add:** Behavioral signals (bounce/dwell/return) feed back into E-E-A-T
  perception — so technical performance and content stickiness now both
  influence E-E-A-T.

### `e-e-a-t-with-about-us-page` chapter

- The About Us chapter is more important than ever in 2026.
- Add specific elements: **certifications, years of experience by named
  staff, real photos, community involvement evidence**.

### `property-audit-quick-wins`

- New checklist item: **"Are bylines real and clickable to author bios?"**
- New checklist item: **"Do testimonials reference specific dates / models
  / circumstances?"**

### `the-core-30-content-strategy`

- The strategic frame is unchanged — but **every page now needs an author**.
- Author should have a bio with E-E-A-T signals.

## Practical 2026 E-E-A-T checklist for local clients

```
[ ] Every content page has a named author with a clickable bio
[ ] Bio page lists: years of experience, certifications, work history
[ ] Original photos on service pages (not stock)
[ ] Testimonials reference specific work / dates
[ ] About Us page includes: founding date, owner name + photo, community involvement, certifications
[ ] Schema markup includes Person + Organization with sameAs links to LinkedIn / industry sites
[ ] Hero claims are quantified ("25 years in business", "1,200+ jobs completed", not "experienced")
[ ] Content reviewed by named expert (if author is not the expert)
[ ] Last-updated date visible on content pages
[ ] No AI-generated content without expert review/byline
```

## Refusal

- No "fake expertise" tactics (made-up author bios, stock-photo "team
  members", fabricated credentials).
- No purchased reviews, no review gating.
- No mass AI-generated content without expert review.
