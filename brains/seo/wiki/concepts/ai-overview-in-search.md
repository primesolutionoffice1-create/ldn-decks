# AI Overview in Search

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> AI Overviews now appear on more local queries; Map Pack pressure is real.
> See **[[2026-ai-overviews-state|2026 AI Overviews State]]** for current state.

Google's AI Overview represents a significant shift in how search results are displayed and consumed. This feature uses artificial intelligence to generate comprehensive answers directly on the search results page, fundamentally changing the SEO landscape.

The AI Overview feature is still evolving, and its impact on SEO continues to develop. While it presents challenges for traditional SEO approaches, understanding how to work around it is crucial for maintaining organic visibility.

When Google's AI Overview appears for a search query, it typically means:

* **The query is informational in nature** \- Questions that can be answered quickly and definitively  
* **Traffic potential is significantly reduced** \- Users get their answer without clicking through to websites  
* **Competition becomes nearly impossible** \- Only extremely high-authority sites get featured

💡 **Key Insight:** If your target keyword triggers an AI Overview, consider pivoting to a different keyword unless you're building topical relevance as part of a larger content strategy.

Who Benefits from AI Overview?

Google claims that websites featured in AI Overview cards receive more click-through traffic than before. However, there's a significant catch:

* Featured sites typically have **domain ratings in the 90s** (like Quora)  
* Small to medium websites rarely qualify for these positions  
* It creates a "winner-takes-all" scenario where one site captures most available traffic

📚 **Related Learning:** Review our lesson on [[key-google-rank-factors|Key Google Rank Factors]] to understand how domain authority affects your ability to compete for these positions.

Checking for AI Overview in Your Keyword Research

Step 1: Test Your Target Keywords

Before committing to a keyword strategy:

1. Search your target keyword on Google  
2. Check if an AI Overview appears  
3. Note which websites are featured in the overview cards

Step 2: Analyze the Competition

When using tools like Ahrefs:

* Look beyond the keyword difficulty score (which can be unreliable)  
* Check the actual domain ratings of ranking sites  
* If most competitors have DR 70+, reconsider your target

⚠️ **Important Note:** Keyword difficulty scores in SEO tools don't account for AI Overview impact. Always manually verify the SERP landscape.

**The Mortgage Calculator Effect**

A cautionary example of Google's SERP features impact:

* Sites that invested heavily in ranking for "mortgage calculator"  
* Google added a calculator directly to the search results  
* **Click-through rates dropped from 80-90% to approximately 67%**  
* Sites lost a third of their traffic overnight

This demonstrates how Google's SERP features can dramatically impact even well-established rankings.

Current Limitations in Tracking

As of now, there are significant tracking challenges:

* **Google Search Console** doesn't track AI Overview clicks  
* **Third-party SEO tools** cannot measure AI Overview performance  
* No reliable way to optimize for or measure success with AI Overview features

This creates a blind spot in your analytics where you can't determine if your content is benefiting from AI Overview exposure.

Strategic Recommendations

1\. Adapt Your Keyword Strategy

* **Avoid purely informational keywords** that trigger AI Overview  
* Focus on transactional and commercial intent keywords  
* Target long-tail keywords with specific user intent

🎯 **Pro Tip:** Check out our module on [Optimizing for ChatGPT](https://www.skool.com/ai-seo-mastery-pro/classroom/63a81f62?md=6990545ad9bc45c884296fdd5d939aa6) to find opportunities for searchers skipping the SERPs entirely and instead asking AI.

2\. Build Topical Authority Strategically

If you must target AI Overview keywords:

* Use them as supporting content to build topical relevance  
* Don't expect direct traffic from these pages  
* Focus on establishing expertise in your niche

3\. Monitor SERP Changes

* Regularly check your target keywords for new AI Overview appearances  
* Be prepared to pivot your strategy as Google expands this feature  
* Focus on keywords where traditional organic results still dominate
