# Which AI Tools to Use

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Model names below are pre-2026. Current lineup is GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.7, Gemini 3.1 Pro.
> See **[[2026-llm-model-lineup|2026 LLM Model Lineup]]** for current state.

Choosing the right AI tool is crucial for efficient SEO work. While several options exist, we'll guide you through our recommended tools and explain when to use each one for maximum effectiveness.

Our Primary Recommendation: Claude

For the majority of SEO tasks covered in this course, **Claude by Anthropic** is our top recommendation. Claude offers two distinct models, each optimized for different types of work:

Claude's Two Models

**🎯 Sonnet 4 \- Your Content Writing Powerhouse**

* Best for: Writing SEO content, creating page copy, generating articles  
* Excels at: Natural language generation, maintaining consistent tone, creating engaging content  
* When to use: Any time you need to create written content for your SEO projects

**📊 Opus 4 \- Your Data Analysis Expert**

* Best for: Technical SEO audits, data analysis, complex problem-solving  
* Excels at: Processing large amounts of data, identifying patterns, comprehensive site audits  
* When to use: Conducting local site technical audits or analyzing SEO performance data

💡 **Quick Tip**: Remember this simple rule \- Sonnet for Stories (content), Opus for Operations (analysis)

Alternative Option: ChatGPT

While Claude is our primary recommendation, ChatGPT remains a valuable tool in certain scenarios. Here's how ChatGPT's models compare:

* **O3**: Similar capabilities to Claude's Opus for auditing and analysis  
* **4.5**: Designed for writing tasks (though Sonnet 4 typically outperforms it)  
* **4.0**: The basic ChatGPT model for general use

When ChatGPT Excels

Despite Claude's advantages, there are three specific areas where ChatGPT offers unique benefits:

**1\. Image Generation** 🎨

* ChatGPT can create images on demand  
* Claude currently lacks this capability  
* Useful for creating visual content for blogs or social media

**2\. Local Business Recommendations** 📍

* ChatGPT will provide actual business recommendations when asked  
* Claude refuses these requests and instead explains how to evaluate businesses  
* Essential for competitive research and local SEO analysis

🔗 **Related Learning**: This capability becomes particularly important in our lesson on "[[llm-seo|Optimizing for ChatGPT]]" where we explore how AI makes local recommendations.

**3\. Voice Functionality** 🎙️

* ChatGPT offers robust voice chat features  
* Perfect for brainstorming sessions while multitasking  
* Can generate written notes from voice conversations  
* Ideal for capturing ideas on the go

Our Recommended Setup

For serious SEO professionals, we maintain subscriptions to both services. Here's why:

1. **Claude Pro** for primary content creation and technical audits  
2. **ChatGPT Plus** for image generation, local business research, and voice brainstorming

This dual-tool approach ensures you're never limited by a single platform's constraints and can always use the best tool for each specific task.
