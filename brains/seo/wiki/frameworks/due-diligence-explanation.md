# Due Diligence Explanation

In local SEO, understanding competition levels is crucial for developing effective strategies and setting realistic expectations with clients. This lesson will teach you how to quickly assess whether a local market is low, medium, or high competition.

Competition in local SEO is determined by two primary factors:

1. **The Service Type** \- Some services are inherently more competitive (e.g., personal injury lawyers vs. house painters)  
2. **The Population of the Area** \- Larger cities typically mean more competition

💡 **Note:** A personal injury lawyer in a small town can be more competitive than a plumber in a large city. Always consider both factors together.

What to Look For: Key Competition Indicators

1\. Review Count in the Map Pack

The number of reviews is your first visual indicator of competition:

* **High Competition:** 200+ reviews (often 500-6,000+)  
* **Medium Competition:** 15-100 reviews  
* **Low Competition:** Under 15 reviews

⚠️ **Important:** While reviews aren't a major ranking signal, they massively impact click-through rates. If competitors have 900 reviews and you have 4, even ranking won't bring calls.

2\. Organic Rankings of Map Pack Businesses

This is your most reliable competition indicator:

* **High Competition:** Map pack businesses also rank on page 1 of organic results  
* **Medium Competition:** Map pack businesses rank on pages 2-4 of organic results  
* **Low Competition:** Map pack businesses don't rank organically at all

3\. Website Presence

Check if the top 3 map pack businesses have websites:

* **High Competition:** All have well-optimized websites  
* **Medium Competition:** All have websites, but not all are optimized  
* **Low Competition:** 1-2 businesses don't even have websites

Real-World Examples

High Competition: Plumber in Chicago

* Reviews: 900, 200, 6,000+  
* Organic Rankings: Top map businesses also dominate page 1 organic results  
* National franchises present (e.g., Roto-Rooter)

Medium Competition: Plumber in Biloxi

* Reviews: 13, 43, 2,700 (outlier)  
* Organic Rankings: Map businesses found on pages 2-5  
* All have websites but varying optimization levels

Low Competition: Plumber in Soshur/Long Beach, MS

* Reviews: 7, 12, 1,277  
* Organic Rankings: Only one business appears organically  
* Two of three businesses don't have websites

The Geographic Ranking Challenge

Here's a critical insight: Even a business with 2,700 reviews struggles to rank in nearby cities where they don't have a physical address.

🗺️ **Case Study:** Ball Plumbing has 2,700 reviews and ranks in Biloxi but can't crack the top 3 in nearby Long Beach, where businesses with 7-12 reviews and no websites dominate.

This demonstrates why **"Geographical Relevance"** (covered in another lesson) is so crucial \- you can't simply overpower local competition from another city, no matter how strong your SEO.

What This Means for Your Strategy

* **Low Competition:** Focus on basic optimization, can achieve results quickly  
* **Medium Competition:** Need solid Core 30 content and consistent link building  
* **High Competition:** Requires comprehensive strategy, significant content, and patience
