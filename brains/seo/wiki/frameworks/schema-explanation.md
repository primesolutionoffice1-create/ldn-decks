# Schema Explanation

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> Use industry-specific subtypes (Plumber, Restaurant, Attorney) over generic LocalBusiness.
> See **[[2026-schema-modernization|2026 Schema Modernization for Local SEO]]** for current state.

Schema markup is one of the quickest wins in SEO—it's incredibly fast to implement and helps search engines understand your content better. Despite being easy to overlook, there's no reason why every single URL on your domain shouldn't have schema markup.
