# The 3 Phases of Local SEO

![[three-phases-of-local-seo.svg]]

Local SEO success follows a strategic three-phase approach that builds your website's authority systematically. Most businesses achieve excellent rankings before completing all three phases, making this a highly efficient methodology.

Your local SEO consists of:

1. **Phase 1: Core 30** \- Building foundational pages  
2. **Phase 2: Topical Relevance** \- Establishing expertise  
3. **Phase 3: Geographic Relevance** \- Expanding your reach

The beauty of this system? Many businesses rank in the top 3 positions across their service area just by completing Phase 1\. You'll monitor your progress using rank maps, moving to the next phase only if needed.

![[images/image14.png]]  
Phase 1: The Core 30 Foundation

The Core 30 forms the backbone of your local SEO strategy. This phase involves creating dedicated pages for every category and service listed on your Google Business Profile (GBP).

What Makes Up the Core 30?

* **One URL per GBP category** (e.g., "Plumber Houston")  
* **One URL per GBP service** (e.g., "Water Heater Replacement Houston")  
* **Essential pages**: About Us, Contact Us  
* **Total**: Approximately 30 pages for most local businesses

Key Implementation Rules

Each Core 30 page requires:

* **Target keyword formula**: \[Service/Category\] \+ \[City Name\]  
* **External backlink**: Every URL needs at least one quality external link  
* **Strategic internal linking**: Proper structure connecting related pages

Monitoring Progress

Set up rank tracking immediately and watch your local rank map as you build. When you see your map turning green (positions 1-3), you're achieving success. Many clients rank well before completing all 30 pages\!

Phase 2: Building Topical Relevance

If your rank map shows limited green areas after Core 30 completion, you need more topical relevance. This phase helps Google trust your expertise in your service area.

When Do You Need Phase 2?

Look at your rank map:

* **Less than 1/3 green**: You need topical relevance  
* **No top 3 rankings near your location**: Definite sign to proceed with Phase 2  
* **Common scenarios**: Personal injury attorneys, competitive markets like Houston

How to Build Topical Relevance

1. **Add FAQ sections** to existing pages  
2. **Answer semantically relevant questions** (50-100 words each)  
3. **Create long-form content** (500+ words) for deeper answers  
4. **Link strategically** between short answers and detailed pages

💡 **Pro Tip**: Don't use exact "People Also Ask" questions anymore. Instead, have AI rewrite them semantically to avoid duplicate content issues while maintaining relevance.

Example Rank Map \- Needs Topical Relevance

When you see no green (1-3 rankings) even at your business location, Google doesn't yet trust your expertise. This calls for topical relevance building.

Phase 3: Expanding Geographic Relevance

Most engagements jump directly from Core 30 to geographic relevance, skipping Phase 2\. This phase extends your ranking radius to cover your entire service area.

When to Implement Phase 3

Your rank map shows:

* **Strong rankings (1-3) near your location**  
* **Positions 4-6 in outer areas**  
* **Clear geographic boundaries** where rankings drop

Geographic Content Strategy

1. **Identify target areas**: Find positions 4, 5, or 6 on your rank map  
2. **Research landmarks**: Locate neighborhoods, parks, intersections near those positions  
3. **Create location-specific content**: \[Primary Service\] \+ \[Geographic Landmark\]  
   * Example: "Water Heater Replacement River Oaks"  
   * Example: "Plumber Near I-10 and 610 Interchange"

🤖 **AI Assistance**: Use the AI prompts covered in later lessons to help identify geographical landmarks and create location-specific content

Example Rank Map \- Ready for Geographic Relevance

When you see lots of 1s, 2s, and 3s near your location but 4s, 5s, and 6s farther out, Google trusts your expertise but needs confirmation you serve those distant areas.

Strategic Implementation Tips

* **Always start with Core 30** \- This alone often achieves your goals  
* **Skip to geographic relevance** if you have good center rankings but poor outer coverage  
* **Add topical relevance** only when lacking authority everywhere

Link Building Reminder

Every new URL across all phases requires at least one external link. This remains constant whether you're building Core 30 pages, topical relevance content, or geographic pages.
