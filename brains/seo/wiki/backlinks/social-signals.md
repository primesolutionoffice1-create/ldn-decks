# Social Signals

Social signals refer to the web links that direct users to your site from social media platforms, such as Facebook, Twitter, or LinkedIn. These occur when social media users share a link to your website, thereby signaling to others that your content is valuable or worth checking out. While social signals do not directly influence search engine rankings, they play a crucial role in demonstrating that your website is active and engaged in social media interactions. This activity helps to establish the authenticity and legitimacy of your site, making it appear more credible to both users and search engines.

Creating a vibrant social media presence can make the links to your site appear more natural. If you are actively engaged in acquiring genuine backlinks, it’s likely that your efforts will naturally result in a variety of social signals as well.
