# LLM SEO

> [!warning] 2026 freshness layer
> AI Overview citation patterns + coverage materially expanded in 2026.
> See **[[2026-ai-overviews-state|2026 AI Overviews State]]** for current state.

Understanding how ChatGPT evaluates and recommends businesses is crucial for optimizing your local SEO strategy in an AI-driven search landscape. While ChatGPT and Google share similar foundational goals, their methods and priorities differ significantly.

Both ChatGPT and Google face the same core problem that search engines have been solving for decades: **determining whether a third-party website or business is trustworthy based solely on information available on the internet.**

Their shared goal is simple—keep users engaged with their platform. Google wants you to stay on Google-owned properties as long as possible, while ChatGPT wants to keep you actively chatting. Neither platform cares about your personal identity; they're focused on validating whether enough trusted sources confirm that your business is legitimate.

💡 **Key Insight**: ChatGPT often references information that's years old, as long as it's relevant to answering the user's question. This means your foundational content remains valuable longer than with traditional Google SEO.

Key Differences: Data Sources and Crawling

Google's Approach

* Actively crawls the entire internet  
* Prioritizes recency and fresh content  
* Updates rankings based on real-time information

ChatGPT's Approach

* **Does not crawl the internet independently**  
* Pulls from Bing API for search results  
* Relies on pre-trained data (up to 2023\)  
* Can generate real-time search results through Bing  
* **Recency is less important**—will reference older content if it's relevant

⏩⏩ **Related Lesson:** [[llm-seo|Data Source: ChatGPT VS. Google]]

Semantic Similarity vs. Exact Match Keywords

This is where ChatGPT fundamentally differs from Google's approach:

Google's Method

* Heavily relies on exact match keywords  
* Uses semantic similarity as a supporting factor

ChatGPT's Method

* **Ignores exact match keywords almost entirely**  
* Focuses primarily on semantic similarity  
* Reads and analyzes the actual content and meaning

Practical Example: Review Analysis

When ChatGPT reads reviews about your plumbing business, it doesn't look for phrases like "best plumber" (which feels spammy). Instead, it identifies semantic patterns like:

* "The plumber was **fast**"  
* "Left everything **clean**"  
* "**Well-priced** service"  
* "**Professional** and courteous"

Reviews mentioning similar qualities and experiences are much more likely to result in ChatGPT recommending that business.

Review Sources and Analysis

Google's Focus

* Primarily relies on Google Reviews  
* Considers review quantity and ratings

ChatGPT's Comprehensive Approach

* Reads **all Google Reviews** thoroughly  
* Analyzes reviews from **every discoverable platform**  
* Focuses on review content quality and consistency

Brand Mentions vs. Backlinks

This represents one of the most significant differences between Google and ChatGPT optimization:

Google (Traditional Citations)

* Requires actual **links** to your website  
* Values backlinks from high-authority domains  
* Link quality and anchor text matter

ChatGPT (Brand Mentions)

* Only needs **business name mentions**  
* No linking required  
* Focuses on mentions in high-trust environments:  
  * Reddit discussions  
  * Local news articles  
  * Community forums  
  * Quora answers  
  * Nextdoor posts

⚡ **Game Changer**: You don't need websites to link to you for ChatGPT optimization—you just need them to mention your business name in trusted environments.

The Critical Role of Schema

Schema markup is important for Google, but it's **even more critical** for ChatGPT optimization.

Why Schema Matters More for ChatGPT

* ChatGPT is much more likely to cite businesses using **Local Business Schema**  
* Clear, structured answers to service-related questions embedded in schema  
* Helps ChatGPT understand and categorize your business accurately

The Relationship Between Google and ChatGPT

While these platforms operate differently, they're interconnected:

**Google rankings serve as a trust factor for ChatGPT.** Businesses that rank well on Google are more likely to be recommended by ChatGPT. This means your existing Google SEO efforts aren't wasted—they actually support your ChatGPT optimization.

The 80/20 Rule

Most strategies that work for Google SEO will also help with ChatGPT recommendations. The key is understanding the **additional optimizations** that specifically target ChatGPT's unique evaluation methods.

Understanding the fundamental differences between ChatGPT and Google's data sources is crucial for your local SEO strategy. These platforms evaluate businesses through entirely different lenses, requiring distinct optimization approaches.

**Google heavily prioritizes fresh content**, especially for news and trending topics. This freshness obsession influences rankings even for established local businesses.

**ChatGPT rarely cares about freshness** unless you explicitly ask it to search online. It primarily relies on its pre-trained knowledge base, valuing comprehensive information regardless of publication date.

Trust Signals: Links vs. Mentions

The platforms evaluate trust through **completely different mechanisms**. Google's trust system centers on high-quality backlinks as the primary currency, supplemented by user engagement signals like time on site and bounce rate, plus brand search volume as key ranking factors.

ChatGPT revolutionizes trust evaluation by prioritizing **citations and mentions over links**. Your business name appearing on authoritative sites builds credibility—no hyperlink required. Schema markup carries significantly more weight for ChatGPT, and it analyzes review content from every accessible platform for context and sentiment, not just keywords.

💡 **Key Insight**: Google demands actual links for authority. ChatGPT values mere mentions on reputable sites, fundamentally changing digital PR strategy.

Data Collection Methods

**Google operates as a relentless crawler**, constantly indexing and updating its understanding of the web. It evaluates individual URLs, tracks real-time changes, and incorporates brand search volume as a ranking factor.

**ChatGPT uses three primary data sources**: the Bing Search API for real-time queries, pre-trained data through 2023, and live website search (though inconsistently applied). This mixed approach means ChatGPT's knowledge combines historical data with selective real-time updates.

⚠️ **Critical Warning**: ChatGPT sometimes claims to search but actually uses heuristics (educated guesses) instead. Don't rely solely on your website—distribute information widely.

What ChatGPT Completely Ignores

**ChatGPT shows zero interest in traditional SEO power metrics**. Backlinks mean nothing—your DR 90 link profile carries no weight. Exact match domains provide no advantage. Page speed and Core Web Vitals are completely irrelevant since ChatGPT often works from cached data.

Your perfectly optimized, lightning-fast website **won't help in ChatGPT** if nobody mentions your business online. This represents a fundamental shift from Google's technical evaluation to ChatGPT's reputation-based assessment.

The Bing Factor

Since ChatGPT connects directly to Bing's API, a **complete Bing Places listing is essential**. Most local SEOs ignore Bing's 3-6% market share, but **neglecting Bing means invisibility to ChatGPT**. This unexpected development makes Bing optimization suddenly critical for future-proofing your local presence.

Related lesson: Review [[gbp-overview|**Types of GBP**]] to understand business profiles across platforms.

Building ChatGPT Influence

Directory listings become critical trust signals—ChatGPT scans for **consistent information** across the web, not just links. Every directory mention reinforces your business's legitimacy and relevance.

Reviews matter differently between platforms. Google wants keyword-rich reviews mentioning specific services and locations. ChatGPT **actually understands review content**, analyzing the customer experience described. A detailed service description beats keyword stuffing every time.

Unlinked mentions count significantly. Blog posts, news articles, and forum discussions mentioning your business name build authority without needing links. Reddit discussions about your business matter because ChatGPT recognizes authentic community conversations as trust signals.

Schema is king for ChatGPT optimization. While Google uses schema to understand content, ChatGPT **weights it far more heavily**. Comprehensive implementation is non-negotiable for AI visibility.

**⏩⏩ Related lesson:** Maximize schema with [[llm-schema|Creating Even More Schema]] for advanced strategies.

Real-World Impact

You could **rank \#1 on Google** yet be **invisible to ChatGPT** if you lack a Bing listing, have no forum or blog mentions, focus on keyword-stuffed instead of authentic reviews, or skimp on schema markup. These oversights create a massive blind spot in your online presence.

Conversely, **modest Google rankings with strong community presence** can make you ChatGPT's top recommendation. A business with widespread mentions, detailed customer reviews, and comprehensive schema often outperforms technically superior competitors in AI recommendations.
