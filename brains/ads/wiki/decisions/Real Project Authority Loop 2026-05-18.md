# Real Project Authority Loop 2026-05-18

## Compiled Truth

For LDN/Loudoun Decks, local dominance should be built from real project evidence before scaling paid media or publishing large volumes of generic SEO content.

This is a stronger strategy than "more SEO" because premium deck leads are influenced by local trust, visual proof, recent reviews, Maps engagement, branded search, and entity consistency. Google Maps, Apple Maps, and AI engines can all benefit when each completed project creates multiple real, consistent, local proof assets.

## Operating Loop

For every completed qualified project, create a reusable evidence package:

1. 10-20 real project photos.
2. 1 compliant review request.
3. 1 Google Business Profile post.
4. 1 Apple Maps photo upload candidate.
5. 1 Pinterest pin or visual social post.
6. 1 project/case-study draft or showcase-page draft.
7. 1 short video candidate.
8. 1 verified city/service mention.
9. 1 internal link opportunity from the relevant service or city page.
10. 1 lead-quality note for future ads and SEO targeting.

## Apple / GBP Photo Mix

Recommended 6-image mix for first Maps proof pass:

- 2 composite deck photos.
- 1 covered deck or screened outdoor living photo.
- 1 stairs/railings detail.
- 1 night lighting shot.
- 1 luxury entertaining / wide outdoor living shot.

This mix is better than uploading only generic deck photos because it broadens semantic service coverage across composite decks, covered decks, railings, lighting, and premium outdoor living.

## Review Request Standard

Do not ask customers only to "leave a review." Ask for honest experience in a way that naturally produces useful detail:

- What material they chose.
- What problem they were solving.
- How they use the deck now.
- What the planning/build experience felt like.
- Whether permits, HOA, communication, cleanup, or craftsmanship stood out.

Do not force keywords, offer incentives, or gate by sentiment.

## GBP Posting Cadence

Use 1 Google Business Profile post per week maximum unless there is a specific seasonal or project reason.

Preferred rotation:

- Week 1: real project proof.
- Week 2: before/after or replacement story.
- Week 3: seasonal tip.
- Week 4: permit/HOA or material-selection insight.

Avoid turning GBP into a spam marketing feed.

## Tracking Upgrade

The current strategy needs better call-source separation before aggressive scaling.

Recommended future tracking design:

- Separate call tracking for Google Ads calls.
- Separate call tracking for organic/SEO calls.
- Separate call tracking for GBP if allowed without NAP risk.
- Separate Apple Maps call/source monitoring where technically feasible.
- Use CallRail or Twilio-style forwarding only after NAP/citation risk is reviewed.

Because local SEO depends on NAP consistency, tracking numbers must be implemented carefully and approval-gated.
