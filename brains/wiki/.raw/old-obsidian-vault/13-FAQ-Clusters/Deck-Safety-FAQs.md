## FAQ

### How do I know if my deck is structurally unsafe?
Loose railings, shaking movement, sinking posts, ledger separation, soft framing, and visible rot may indicate structural safety concerns.

### Why does my deck shake when people walk on it?
Deck movement may result from weak framing, undersized beams, loose connections, or footing settlement.

### Are loose railings dangerous?
Yes. Loose railings may create serious fall hazards and often require immediate structural inspection.

### What causes deck stairs to become unstable?
Improper framing, weak stringers, moisture damage, loose fasteners, and footing movement commonly contribute to unstable deck stairs.

### Can water damage weaken a deck?
Yes. Long-term moisture exposure may damage framing, fasteners, ledger boards, and structural supports.

### How often should a deck be inspected?
Many homeowners benefit from periodic deck inspections, especially for older wood decks exposed to weather and moisture.

### Do composite decks still require structural inspections?
Yes. Composite deck boards still rely on structural framing systems that may require inspection and maintenance.

### What are common signs of deck framing failure?
Sagging boards, cracking sounds, movement, loose railings, soft wood, and uneven stairs are common warning signs.
