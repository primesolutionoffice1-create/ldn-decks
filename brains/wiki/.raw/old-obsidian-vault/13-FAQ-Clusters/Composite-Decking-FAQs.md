## FAQ

### Is composite decking worth the money?
Composite decking often provides lower maintenance, better moisture resistance, and longer lifespan compared to traditional wood decking.

### Does composite decking get hot?
Darker composite boards can retain more heat, especially in direct sunlight.

### How long does composite decking last?
Many composite decking systems last 25–50 years depending on installation quality and environmental exposure.

### Does composite decking fade?
Modern capped composite decking typically offers improved fade resistance compared to older generations.

### Is PVC decking better than composite decking?
PVC decking often offers stronger moisture resistance while composite decking may provide more natural wood aesthetics.

### Can composite decking be repaired?
Yes. Some damaged boards can be replaced individually depending on framing condition and board availability.
