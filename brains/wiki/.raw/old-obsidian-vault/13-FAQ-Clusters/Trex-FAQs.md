## FAQ

### Is Trex decking low maintenance?
Trex decking generally requires less maintenance than traditional wood decking because staining and sealing are not usually required.

### Does Trex decking fade?
Modern Trex collections offer improved fade resistance compared to older composite boards.

### Is Trex slippery when wet?
Many Trex boards provide good traction, but moisture, pollen, and debris may affect slip resistance.

### Can Trex decking be pressure washed?
Yes, but manufacturer cleaning recommendations should be followed carefully.

### How long does Trex decking last?
Many Trex systems are designed for long-term durability and may last several decades.
