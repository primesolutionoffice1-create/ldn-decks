## FAQ

### How do I know if my deck is unsafe?
Loose railings, shaking movement, wood rot, ledger separation, and sinking posts may indicate structural safety concerns.

### Can an old deck be repaired instead of replaced?
Some decks can be resurfaced or repaired if the framing remains structurally sound.

### What causes deck rot?
Moisture exposure, poor ventilation, water intrusion, and inadequate flashing commonly contribute to wood rot.

### How long do deck repairs take?
Repair timelines vary depending on framing damage, permits, material availability, and structural complexity.

### Can composite decks be repaired?
Yes. Certain composite deck boards and railing systems may be replaceable individually.
