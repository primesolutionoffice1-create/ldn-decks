## FAQ

### Do I need a permit to build a deck in Virginia?
Most elevated or attached decks in Virginia require permits and inspections depending on structure size, height, stairs, and framing.

### How long does a deck permit take?
Permit timelines vary by county and project complexity, but many residential deck permits take several business days to several weeks.

### Are deck inspections required?
Most permitted decks require footing, framing, and final inspections before approval.

### Do HOA approvals replace permits?
No. HOA approvals and county permits are separate requirements.

### What happens if a deck is built without permits?
Unpermitted decks may create inspection problems, fines, resale complications, and structural liability concerns.

### Do composite decks require permits?
Yes. Composite decking projects still require permits when structural work is involved.
