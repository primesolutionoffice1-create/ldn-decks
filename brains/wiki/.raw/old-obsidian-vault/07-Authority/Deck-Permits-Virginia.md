## Permit Context

Deck projects connected to [[Deck-Builder-Northern-Virginia]] often require local permit review depending on scope, structure, height, attachment method, stairs, railing, and jurisdiction.

Permit requirements connect directly with [[Virginia-Deck-Code-Guide]], [[Deck-Footings-Virginia]], and [[HOA-Deck-Rules]] because most deck projects involve both building-code compliance and local approval rules.

Material selection also matters because composite and PVC deck projects may connect with [[Deck-Materials-Authority-Guide]], [[Composite-Decking-Guide]], [[Trex-Decking-Guide]], and [[TimberTech-Guide]].
