## Decision Criteria
- Upfront cost versus lifetime maintenance.
- Heat resistance and comfort.
- Moisture resistance and rot risk.
- Warranty terms and installation requirements.
- Color stability and design options.
- Fit for Northern Virginia weather and HOA expectations.
