## Water Damage Context

Water damage is one of the most common structural problems connected with
[[Deck-Repair-Hub]]
and
[[Deck-Repair-Services]]
because long-term moisture exposure can weaken framing, railings, stairs, and deck surfaces.

Structural repair discussions frequently reference
[[Deck-Framing-and-Accessories]],
[[Deck-Footings-Virginia]],
and
[[Virginia-Deck-Code-Guide]]
because improper flashing, weak drainage,
and framing exposure often lead to structural deterioration.

Moisture-related maintenance planning commonly connects with
[[Composite-Decking-Guide]],
[[Deck-Materials-Authority-Guide]],
and
[[Under-Deck-Drainage]]
because composite and PVC systems are frequently selected to reduce long-term moisture issues.

Luxury outdoor living projects often integrate drainage planning through
[[Covered-Decks]]
and
[[Outdoor-Living-Spaces]]
to improve water management and long-term deck durability.

## Common Signs of Water Damage
- Soft deck boards
- Spongy framing
- Loose railings
- Mold or mildew growth
- Cracked boards
- Rusted fasteners
- Ledger separation
- Water stains beneath deck

## Common Causes
- Poor drainage
- Incorrect flashing
- Improper fasteners
- Standing water
- Inadequate ventilation
- Aging wood framing
- Improper ledger attachment

## Water Damage Repair Considerations
- Structural inspection
- Framing replacement
- Board replacement
- Flashing upgrades
- Drainage improvements
- Composite resurfacing
- Permit inspections
