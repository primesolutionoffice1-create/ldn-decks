## Common Signs
- Soft deck boards
- Dark discoloration
- Cracking wood
- Mold growth
- Spongy walking surfaces
- Loose fasteners

## Common Causes
- Poor drainage
- Water pooling
- Lack of ventilation
- Aging wood
- Incorrect flashing
