## Trex Colors and Quality

### Common Trex Colors
- Foggy Wharf
- Havana Gold
- Spiced Rum
- Island Mist
- Tiki Torch
- Rocky Harbor

### Quality Characteristics
- Strong color consistency
- Durable capped composite shell
- Good fade protection
- Wide product availability
- Mid-to-premium quality tiers

### Potential Drawbacks
- Some darker boards retain heat
- Lower collections may scratch easier
- Premium lines increase project costs

## TimberTech Colors and Quality

### Common TimberTech Colors
- Coastline
- Dark Hickory
- Weathered Teak
- English Walnut
- Maritime Gray
- Pecan

### Quality Characteristics
- Premium wood-look aesthetics
- Advanced PVC options available
- Strong moisture resistance
- Excellent texture realism
- Luxury-focused collections

### Potential Drawbacks
- Higher material pricing
- Some premium boards require careful installation
- Availability can vary

## Fiberon Colors and Quality

### Common Fiberon Colors
- Cabin
- Cottage
- Tuscan Villa
- Graphite
- Burnt Umber

### Quality Characteristics
- Good mid-range value
- Multiple color families
- Lower maintenance
- Competitive pricing

### Potential Drawbacks
- Fewer ultra-premium collections
- Some product lines less realistic
- Contractor familiarity varies

## Color Selection Considerations

### Lighter Colors
- Lower heat retention
- Modern appearance
- Better barefoot comfort
- Popular for sunny decks

### Darker Colors
- Rich luxury appearance
- Strong contrast
- Higher heat absorption
- More visible dust and pollen
