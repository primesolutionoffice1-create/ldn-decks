## Framing Context

Structural deck framing is one of the most important components connected with
[[Deck-Builder-Northern-Virginia]]
because framing quality directly impacts safety,
lifespan, inspections, and long-term deck performance.

Deck framing projects frequently connect with
[[Deck-Footings-Virginia]],
[[Virginia-Deck-Code-Guide]],
and
[[Deck-Permits-Virginia]]
because structural framing must comply with inspection and load-bearing requirements.

Repair and replacement projects often relate to
[[Deck-Repair-Services]],
[[Deck-Replacement]],
and
[[Deck-Repair-vs-Replacement]]
when joists, ledger boards, or support posts show damage.

Composite decking installations commonly reference
[[Composite-Deck-Installation]],
[[Composite-Decking-Guide]],
and
[[Deck-Materials-Authority-Guide]]
because premium decking systems require stable framing structures.
