## Primary Semantic Entity

Daily content workflows support ongoing topical authority, semantic expansion, AI visibility, and local SEO growth.

## Daily Workflow

1. Publish one new page
2. Add internal links
3. Connect page to one master hub
4. Add FAQ section
5. Add GEO relevance if applicable
6. Add comparison links
7. Add gallery links
8. Re-import Smart Connections

## Priority Content Types

- GEO pages
- FAQs
- comparisons
- repair topics
- luxury outdoor living
- galleries
- case studies
