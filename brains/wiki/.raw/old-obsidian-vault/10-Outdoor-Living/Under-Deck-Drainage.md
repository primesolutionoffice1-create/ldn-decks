## Drainage Context

Homeowners researching
[[Deck-Builder-Northern-Virginia]]
often install under-deck drainage systems to create dry storage,
covered patios, and more functional outdoor living environments beneath elevated decks.

Drainage systems commonly connect with
[[Covered-Decks]],
[[Outdoor-Living-Spaces]],
and
[[Deck-Framing-and-Accessories]]
because framing layout and water routing must be planned together.

Composite deck projects frequently reference
[[Composite-Deck-Installation]],
[[Deck-Materials-Authority-Guide]],
and premium decking systems from
[[Trex-Decking-Guide]]
and
[[TimberTech-Guide]].

Moisture management and structural protection often relate to
[[Deck-Repair-Services]]
and
[[Deck-Footings-Virginia]]
because long-term water exposure can damage framing systems.
