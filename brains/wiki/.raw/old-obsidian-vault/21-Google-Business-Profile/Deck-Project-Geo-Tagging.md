## Primary Semantic Entity

Deck project geo tagging connects project photos, city relevance, county authority, service pages, case studies, and Google Business Profile visibility.

## Geo Tagging Purpose

Each real project should reinforce:
- city relevance
- service relevance
- material relevance
- project proof
- local trust
- photo authority

## Project Data Fields

For every project, record:

- City
- County
- Neighborhood if appropriate
- Project type
- Material
- Brand
- Railing type
- Permit jurisdiction
- HOA involvement
- Before photo
- After photo
- Gallery URL
- Case study URL
- Google Business Profile photo caption
