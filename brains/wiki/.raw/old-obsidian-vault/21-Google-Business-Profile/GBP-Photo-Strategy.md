## Photo Context

Photos should connect real project proof with semantic visual galleries such as [[Deck-Project-Gallery-System]], [[Before-and-After-Projects]], [[Composite-Deck-Color-Gallery]], [[Cable-Railing-Gallery]], and [[Luxury-Outdoor-Living-Gallery]].

Each GBP photo should ideally include:
- project city
- project type
- material
- service
- short caption
- before/after context if applicable
