## Primary Semantic Entity

Deck inspections identify structural risks, unsafe conditions, water damage, railing movement, framing issues, and repair or replacement recommendations.

## Inspection Areas

- framing
- footings
- ledger attachment
- railings
- stairs
- fasteners
- moisture damage
- structural movement
