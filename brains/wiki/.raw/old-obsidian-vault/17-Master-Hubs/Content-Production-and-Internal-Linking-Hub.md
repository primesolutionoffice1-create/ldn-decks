## Content Production Rules

Every new content page should connect to:

1. One master hub
2. One service entity
3. One authority page
4. One comparison page
5. One FAQ cluster
6. One buyer-intent page
7. One local page where relevant

## Internal Linking Formula

A strong page should include:

- Parent hub link
- Related service links
- Related location links
- Related authority links
- Related comparison links
- Related FAQ links
- Related conversion page links
