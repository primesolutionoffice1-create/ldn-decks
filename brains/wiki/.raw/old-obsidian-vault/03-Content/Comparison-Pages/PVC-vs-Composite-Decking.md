# PVC vs Composite Decking

## Role
PVC and composite decking both target low-maintenance buyers, but they differ in heat behavior, capstock, weight, moisture resistance, cost, and brand positioning. This page should clarify when each material is the stronger fit.

## Decision Criteria
- Upfront cost and lifetime maintenance.
- Heat, fading, moisture, and warranty considerations.
- Visual design fit for Northern Virginia homes.
- Best-fit buyer profile and project type.
