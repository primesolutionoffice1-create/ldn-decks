# Operational Launch Plan: 2026-05-16

## Goal

Use the completed LDN Decks wiki as the operating brain for SEO, content, local SEO, Google Ads, AI visibility, and conversion work.

## Operating Rule

Do not publish or execute strategy directly from archived notes. First validate against:

- Current ldndecks.com pages.
- Google Search Console.
- Google Ads.
- Live SERPs.
- Google Business Profile.
- Real project photos, reviews, and proof.

## First 5 Workstreams

1. SEO: turn [[seo-command-center|SEO Command Center]] into a weekly page-priority queue.
2. Content: use [[content-calendar|Content Calendar]] to choose the next publishable page or update.
3. Local SEO: use [[local-seo-tracker|Local SEO Tracker]] to prioritize city/service combinations.
4. Google Ads: use [[google-ads-tracker|Google Ads Tracker]] to audit PMAX, brand overlap, negatives, and landing-page relevance.
5. AI Visibility: use [[ai-visibility-tracker|AI Visibility Tracker]] to convert FAQ, schema, entity, and proof assets into answer-ready content.

## Week 1 Actions

- Validate top money pages: Deck Builder Northern Virginia, Composite Deck Installation, Deck Repair, Deck Resurfacing.
- Pick 3 local pages to strengthen: Ashburn, Fairfax, Arlington.
- Build or update one conversion-focused landing page from [[LDN Decks Google Ads Audit]].
- Review internal links from city pages to service, county permit, proof, and estimate pages.
- Select 10 FAQ answers for schema and AI visibility review.

## Output Cadence

- Daily: update [[hot]] with the active focus.
- Weekly: update [[dashboard|Dashboard]] and [[seo-command-center|SEO Command Center]].
- After every meaningful change: commit and push to GitHub plus backup-local.
