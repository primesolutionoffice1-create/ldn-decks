# LDN Decks Command Center

## Source Summary

This source is the weekly operations dashboard for the old LDN Decks brain. It turns the vault from a passive note collection into an operating system with recurring priorities, active focus, hub navigation, and maintenance links.

## Key Facts

- Weekly priorities include reviewing content gaps, shipping one revenue or service page improvement, adding internal links from hubs, checking graph health, and updating analytics/conversion notes after campaigns or SEO work.
- Active focus is building ldndecks.com into the strongest Northern Virginia deck builder authority across organic search, local search, AI answers, paid search, and conversion flows.
- Operating hubs cover SEO, AI search, local and GBP, services, content, authority, ads/revenue, measurement, and visual proof.
- Maintenance links include graph legend, master canvas, readiness audit, start page, orphan cleanup, task operations, evidence library, reports, and editorial backlog.
