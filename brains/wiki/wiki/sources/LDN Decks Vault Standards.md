# LDN Decks Vault Standards

## Source Summary

This source defines maintenance standards for the old LDN Decks vault: required properties, naming rules, hub linkage, canvas hygiene, and review rhythm.

## Key Facts

- Every durable note should have `type`, `title`, `created`, `updated`, `status`, and `tags`.
- Filenames should be unique so wikilinks resolve without ambiguity.
- Every durable note should link from at least one hub, dashboard, or relationship map.
- The vault should use one intentional master canvas and avoid root-level abandoned canvases.
- Review rhythm: weekly command-center updates, monthly graph cleanup, and quarterly refreshes for service, city, GBP, AI search, ads, and analytics hubs.
