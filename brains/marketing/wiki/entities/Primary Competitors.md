# Primary Competitors

Populated from `competitors-2026-05-11.json` and the 20 legit per-competitor `competitor-kw-*-2026-05-11.json` files. 7 aggregator domains were quarantined; provenance in [[Competitor Landscape Cache]].

## Discovery Method

DataForSEO Labs `competitors_domain` endpoint with three NoVA deck-builder seeds (`top-rated deck builder northern virginia`, `custom deck builder & contractor serving northern virginia`, `trex platinum & timbertech certified deck builders in nova`) at depth 20. 27 domains surfaced; 7 aggregators (yelp/houzz/angi/bestpickreports/northernvirginiamag/trex/locator.timbertech) quarantined to `.raw/sources/dataforseo-aggregators/`. The 20 remaining domains profiled below.

## Strategic Notes

- **Tier 1 is the direct comparison set.** Out-execute on E-E-A-T, Information Gain, and topical depth — see [[Information Gain]] and [[Topical Authority for Niche Sites]].
- **The 5 Tier-1 competitors split the NoVA market**: deckscapesofva (screened porch + pools), battlefielddecks (patio + near-me), deckguru (deck repair), nvdeck (NoVA-state cluster), northerndeckworks (also state cluster). LDN Decks has a foothold in the brand-geo (Loudoun #1) and screened-porch (page 2/3 across all variants) clusters; the strategy is to consolidate those holdings and build into deck-repair, patio, and TimberTech where the SERP is most takeable.
- **Government / aggregator competitors** are uncatchable on raw authority — strategy is to win on local Information Gain, not domain rating.
- **Algorithmic recovery**: some competitors may also be in recovery — opens windows but means SERPs may be unstable.
- **Wrong-geography demotion** matters here: `fenceanddeckconnection.com` looks scary on the spreadsheet but is in Maryland. Do not anchor a strategy to chasing their rankings.
