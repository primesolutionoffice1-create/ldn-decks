## 7. AI Overview tactics

AI Overviews trigger heavily on local-intent queries. Expect AIO on `trex deck builder fairfax`, `screened porch contractor near reston`, `cost of composite deck virginia`, `who installs timbertech near me`. Be a citation-worthy source; do not try to game extraction.

1. **Entity completeness.** Business name, address, phone identical across website, GBP, BBB, Houzz, Trex Locator, TimberTech Locator. AIO cross-references these. Audit lives in Section 3 Lever 2.
2. **FAQ schema — honesty only.** Every service page gets `FAQPage` schema, populated only with real customer questions sourced from CallRail transcripts, GBP Q&A, and the form-submission archive. Fabricated FAQs are detectable and counter-productive.
3. **Passage-level chunking.** Every H2 reads as a standalone extractable answer. Example for `/services/deck-repair/`: H2 "How much does deck repair cost in Northern Virginia" → 60-word direct answer → supporting table below. AIO extracts the paragraph.
4. **Brand mention signals.** Pitch the local-press story per Section 3 Lever 4. Unlinked brand mentions in `northernvirginiamag.com`, `loudountimes.com`, `insidenova.com` count toward AIO entity recognition.
5. **Citation worthiness.** Every verifiable claim attaches a primary source: NoVA permit cost → Loudoun or Fairfax County fee schedule; warranty claim → Trex/TimberTech warranty PDF; cost range → your dated NoVA cost table.

Local-specific AIO levers: named provider on every service page ("Project lead: [name], 15 years NoVA deck-building, Trex Platinum certified") — AIO surfaces named-provider snippets when they exist; review snippets in schema match real reviews on GBP and Yelp; external citations to industry-association pages (NADRA membership if you carry it) signal recognized-local-entity status.

## 8. AI search tactics (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Bing Chat, Google AI Mode)

AI search engines pick citations on **direct-answer-first content, named-author trust, primary-source citability.** Your authentic credentials (Trex Platinum, TimberTech Platinum, 5.0★) need to be legible.

1. **`llms.txt` at site root.** Publish `https://ldndecks.com/llms.txt` listing your top 12 canonical URLs (home + 6 service hubs + 5 location pages) with one-sentence summaries. Add the deck-repair and TimberTech hubs when they ship. Add `llms-full.txt` only when at least 8 pages have refreshed content live.
2. **Direct answer in the first 60 words after the H1.** Every service page. No throat-clearing intros.
3. **Schema completeness only where the page genuinely qualifies.** `LocalBusiness` on every service page; `Service` on every `/services/*` page; `BreadcrumbList` site-wide; `Article` only on blog posts — never on commercial service pages.
4. **Named-author signals.** Author bio block on every authored page; author archive at `/team/<name>/`; LinkedIn linked; author as `sameAs` contact in `Person` schema.
5. **Source citability.** Every numeric claim cites a primary source + date. "TimberTech AZEK Vintage list price as of 2026 Q2: $X per linear foot, source: timbertech.com/products/azek-vintage" beats "TimberTech is premium."

> [!key-insight]
> AI search citation is a long-cycle reward. ChatGPT's index refresh, Perplexity's crawler cadence, and Claude's web search are all slower than Google's. Do not expect AI-search-driven traffic to move in the 30-day window. The compounding shows at month 4-6 when the refreshed pages have been crawled multiple times.

## 10. White-hat guardrails

This is the honesty contract. It is the most important section of this plan because it is the contract you are agreeing to live by. Every recommendation in Sections 1-9 conforms to it. If you ever find a contradiction, the contradiction wins — that recommendation is wrong.

### Forbidden tactics — explicit enumeration

1. **No mass AI content.** No bulk-generated service-area pages, no AI-rewritten guides at scale, no AI-generated FAQ pages populated with hypothetical questions. The deck-repair hub and TimberTech hub are human-written and reviewed by Daniel Agrici before publish.
2. **No link buying.** No paid backlinks, no sponsored editorial dressed as honest review, no link exchanges with other deck builders, no PBN participation, no expired-domain purchases for backlink equity.
3. **No PBNs.** Private blog networks are a manual-action vector and a long-term liability.
4. **No review gating.** Every customer is invited to review. No survey-first-to-filter — Google policy violation; Yelp flags it aggressively. Unhappy customers get a public response, not a hidden review.
5. **No fabricated reviews.** No purchasing, no swaps with other contractors, no employees-as-customers. Local-services suspensions for fake reviews are severe.
6. **No fabricated schema.** `aggregateRating` reflects real Google reviews only. `reviewCount` is the integer Google shows on your GBP profile, not an inflated number.
7. **No fabricated stats.** Every numeric claim cites a primary source or is removed. Industry folklore ("decks add X% home value") gets a real study or gets cut.
8. **No traffic recovery guarantees.** Rankings depend partly on Google's update window — partly out of our hands.
9. **No #1 ranking guarantees.** Targets are top-10 and top-3, outcomes the work *can* produce, not *will* produce.
10. **No "one weird trick" framing, schemes, or "Google doesn't want you to know" copy.**
11. **No cloaking, hidden text, doorway pages.** Served content matches rendered content.
12. **No keyword stuffing in the GBP business name.** "LDN Decks" — not "LDN Decks — Best Trex Deck Builder Northern Virginia". GBP suspends for this.
13. **No fake GBP addresses or virtual-office spam.** Real operating address only. Service-area listings only cover counties you actually service.
14. **No identical content across location pages.** Every `/top-decks-build-near-you/deck-builder-in-<city>/` has unique local content: real projects, neighborhood photos, local-permit references, named on-site lead.
15. **No citations on spam directories.** Tier-1 and industry-specific only.
16. **No claiming a service area you cannot service.** Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William, Alexandria, Arlington — only what LDN Decks actually operates in.
17. **No deceptive comparison content.** The `/trex-vs-timbertech-vs-azek` page is honest about trade-offs. Recommendations follow use case, not affiliate economics.
